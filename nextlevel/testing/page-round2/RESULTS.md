# Page visitor test, round 2 (2026-11-15)

Same 100 model-written personas, page version 5 (first screen states what it is and the price; trades named; sample call; shorter page; no email asked while nothing can be sent). Finds weak points; not market research, not a forecast.

By attention: skimmers who started the quiz 0 then 1 of 19; half-readers 12 then 17 of 50; full readers 22 then 23 of 31.

## Reading it

- The first-screen fix worked on understanding. Skimmers still leave, but now for real reasons they can state: the price is beyond them, they need leads not a receptionist, they already have a tool, they are in the US, their trade is not named. The page is filtering, not confusing.
- The same first-screen lines appear in both the "helped" and "hurt" lists: the price and the plain description attract fits and release non-fits early.
- Would-buy doubled from 2 to 4 (a Mississauga agent, a Calgary agent, a Winnipeg agent and a GTA mortgage broker).

## Still losing people (mentions)

Booking through Instagram 29; who is behind it, no company name, portrait or testimonials 27; languages 23; nothing to hear 19; quiz as the only next step 17; the brief is not sent anywhere 17; price 14; US 14.

## Two who abandoned the quiz

- A leasing agent: the intake speaks of a "new buyer" and a "showing", and 250 minutes is too few for forty rental calls a week.
- An accountant: wanted Reviews (marked "Opening next" with no date) and the waitlist path only asked about phone problems.

## What the page cannot fix

A booking calendar, a demo number or real recording, the registered business name and a portrait, a tested second language, and the first client results.
