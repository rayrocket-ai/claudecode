# Offer test, round 1 (2026-11-15)

100 model-written personas heard the pitch in `offer-pitch.md`. This finds weak points in the offer. It is not market research and not a sales forecast.

## Tally

| Group | Buys | Books a call | Joins waitlist | Passes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 70 agents, founding price ($300 a month) | 0 | 7 | 0 | 63 |
| 70 agents, standard price ($500 + $400 a month) | 0 | 2 | 0 | 68 |
| 30 business owners (waitlist only) | 0 | 0 | 1 (a competitor) | 29 |

- Agents who would pay something: 50 of 70. At $300 or more: 31. At $400 or more: 16. Median of those who would pay: $300.
- Price reaction among agents: fair 29, expensive 18, cannot judge 14, cheap 9.
- Best-fit product for agents: Answer 47, none 22, Nurture 1.
- Average trust in Ray: 2.8 of 5 (agents), 2.4 of 5 (business owners). Would refer: 4 of 100.

## What the offer did not answer (people it mattered to)

| Gap | Count |
|---|---|
| Usage cost, or a cap | 32 |
| Which languages the AI speaks | 26 |
| Call recordings and who holds the data | 22 |
| Waitlist: no date, no price | 17 |
| Legal business name behind Next Level | 14 |
| Which CRMs it connects to | 14 |
| Whether their brokerage allows it | 6 |
| US availability and USD price | 5 |
| Written promise that Ray's team never contacts their callers | 4 |

## The 14 "ready if one question is answered" personas

2 booked a call. 12 passed, each on one unanswered line: an all-in monthly number, a stated usage cost, CRM sync, live listing lookup, mid-call handoff to a person, team pricing, rental screening questions, the AI speaking Dari, a demo number to dial, forwarding when the phone is off, a waitlist date, and a contractor who wanted to buy now and could not.

## Reading it

- Price is not the main objection. 31 of 70 agents would pay $300 or more, yet none bought, because the offer leaves cost, language, privacy and CRM open.
- "Usage is quoted on the call" is the single most expensive sentence in the offer.
- Nobody outside Ray has used it, so the standard price converts almost no one. The founding price is doing its job; the standard price needs founding-client results before it is shown.
- The waitlist with no date and no price converts no real business owner.
