## The pitch

This is everything the persona is told. Nothing else may be assumed.

**Who:** Ray Ahmadi. GTA real estate broker, Ray Homes Real Estate under eXp Realty, 12+ years, about 50 deals a year, multi-year eXp Icon agent. Works in English and Dari. He runs this system on his own business and shows it live on a call. The service is called Next Level, operated by Ray Ahmadi. You get a written agreement before you pay, and it names the registered business and its address.

**New, and why the price is what it is:** Next Level is new. The first 10 clients are its founding clients and there are no client testimonials yet. That is the reason for the founding terms: a lower price, no setup fee and a locked price, in return for being first. Next Level never recruits a client to Ray's brokerage or team. Joining anything of Ray's is not a condition of the price, the service or the support.

**What (available now): Next Level Answer.** An AI receptionist on your business line. It answers every call you miss or cannot take, says it is an AI, asks your qualifying questions (you choose them: buyer or seller, timeline, pre-approval, rental screening and so on), books the appointment into your calendar, and texts you a summary. If a caller asks for a person or it is urgent, it offers a callback and alerts you by text straight away. You keep your number; calls forward only when you do not answer, including when your phone is off, and forwarding is switched off the day you cancel. Ray's team sets it up for you. It only answers calls that come in. It does not place sales calls.

**Founding offer (first 10 clients only):** $300 CAD a month. No setup fee. Price locked while you stay subscribed. Month to month, cancel before the next billing date. Live within 7 days of you supplying what is needed, or the first month is not charged. Feedback calls at day 14 and day 30.

**Usage, as one number:** the $300 includes 250 AI call minutes a month, your phone number, and the summary and alert texts (up to 500 text segments). If a call runs two minutes, 250 minutes is 125 answered calls. Extra minutes are $0.30 CAD each. A part minute counts as a minute and unused minutes do not carry over. Before go-live you set a monthly cap on extra minutes, and it can be zero; the system stops and alerts you if the cap is reached. With the cap at zero, the most you can be billed is $300. With a cap of 100 extra minutes, the most is $330. Minutes used are shown on every invoice, and you see Ray's own line's real usage on the call before you pay.

**Try before you pay:** a demo number is on the page. Dial it before you book anything and hear the AI answer. On the call you dial the live system again and hear it answer as your business would.

**Languages:** English is live today. The platform also lists French, Spanish, Hindi, German, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Russian and Japanese; any of these is switched on at your request and tested with you first. Dari, Farsi, Punjabi, Mandarin, Cantonese, Urdu and Arabic are not promised: if you need one, it is checked against the platform's current list and tested with you during set-up. Ray speaks Dari himself; the AI is not promised in Dari. The rule for every language is the same: it is tested on your own line with you, and if it does not handle your callers to your standard, you do not go live and you are not charged.

**Your callers and your data:** your callers stay yours. No one at Next Level or Ray Homes contacts your callers or uses your contacts, and that is written in the agreement.
- *Where it is held:* your account runs on HighLevel, a US software company. HighLevel says its infrastructure is in the United States, on Google Cloud and Amazon Web Services, and it publishes a SOC 2 Type II report. Your data is not stored in Canada. Next Level is a customer of HighLevel, not a partner or affiliate of it.
- *Who can read a transcript:* you, and the set-up team only while they are setting up your account or fixing something you asked them to fix. The set-up team is named in your agreement. To turn speech into text and back, the call passes through the platform's speech and AI suppliers.
- *Recording:* calls are transcribed so you get the summary. Audio recording is off unless you turn it on, and when it is on the AI says so at the start of the call.
- *Leaving:* your contacts and transcripts are exported to you and deleted from the account within 30 days of the day you leave.

**Your CRM:** every call reaches you as a text and an email summary, so it works beside any CRM from day one. For four CRMs there is also a push into the CRM: after each call the caller is created or updated as a contact or lead, with the summary.
- *Follow Up Boss and BoldTrail (kvCORE):* through Zapier.
- *Lofty and Sierra Interactive:* Zapier lists the same kind of connection; it is tested in your account during set-up week.
- It is one way: from Next Level Answer into your CRM. It does not read or change anything else in your CRM. You authorise it yourself during set-up, and there is no extra charge for it.
- If the push for one of these four does not work in your account during set-up, you can cancel and you are not charged.
- Any other CRM: the text and email summary. Next Level is not affiliated with Zapier or with any CRM company.

**Your brokerage:** you get a one-page summary to show your broker of record before you sign. If your brokerage says no within 14 days, you get your first month back.

**Teams:** one subscription covers one business line. Each extra agent line on the same account is half the monthly price: $150 a month at the founding price, $200 at the standard price. Each extra line adds 125 minutes, and all lines on the account share one pool of minutes.

**Standard price after the first 10:** $500 setup plus $400 a month, with the same 250 minutes and the same $0.30 for extra minutes.

**No cheaper plan:** there is no cheaper plan today. If $300 a month is more than your phone problem is worth, this is not the right service for you yet.

**Opening next, in this order (not available yet):** Reviews ($250 setup, $150 a month). Nurture ($500, $250 a month). Ads ($500, $750 a month, ad spend on your own card). Complete ($1,500, $1,500 a month): all four.

**For businesses that are not real estate agents:**
- *Mortgage brokers, and contractors and renovators:* 3 of the 10 founding spots are open to you now, on the same founding terms as agents: $300 a month, no setup fee, price locked, 250 minutes included. Same quiz, same 15-minute call.
- *Real estate lawyers, accountants and insurance:* Answer opens to you on 1 March 2027 at $500 setup plus $400 a month. Join the waitlist and you get first notice before that date, and one question now: your industry and your biggest phone problem.
- *Any other business:* you can join the waitlist; no date is promised.

**Where:** founding clients are in Canada only, and the price is in Canadian dollars. If you are in the United States, you can join the US list. By 1 March 2027 everyone on the US list is told the US opening date and the price in US dollars. Nothing is sold to US clients before then.

**How you buy:** a short quiz, then a 15-minute call with Ray (evening and weekend times available), then the written agreement, then payment by card.
