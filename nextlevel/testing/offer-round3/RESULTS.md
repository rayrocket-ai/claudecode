# Offer test, round 3 (2026-11-15)

Same 100 model-written personas, pitch `offer-pitch-v3.md`. Finds weak points; not market research, not a forecast.

At the standard price ($500 setup plus $400 a month): buys 0, 1, 2 across the three rounds.

## Who bought in round 3

Five Canadian agents (Mississauga, downtown Toronto leasing, Peterborough cottage country, Calgary, Winnipeg), one GTA mortgage agent and one Etobicoke renovation company owner. Every buyer is in Canada and has a specific missed-call problem.

## What changed the result

- The minute bundle closed the usage question: it fell from 23 mentions to 1.
- Named CRM connections: 9 mentions to 2.
- Opening 3 founding spots to mortgage brokers and contractors produced 2 buyers and 4 call bookings from a group that produced none before.
- Stated limits cost call bookings (19 to 12) and removed calls with people who cannot be served: US agents moved to the US list.

## Lines that hurt most in round 3

- "Dari, Farsi, Punjabi, Mandarin, Cantonese, Urdu and Arabic are not promised" (8)
- "there is no cheaper plan today" (7)
- "Nothing is sold to US clients before then." (7)
- "Your data is not stored in Canada." (6)
- "there are no client testimonials yet" (4)

## Still unanswered

Languages 22; US availability 14; whether HST is added 8; cap and recording behaviour unverified 7; registered business name 6; brokerage approval 6; data location 6.

## Reading it

- Specific numbers convert. Buyers went 0, 3, 7 as vagueness came out.
- The remaining losses are real limits of the product today (languages, US, data location) and the absence of client results. More wording will not move them.
- The biggest untested lever is languages: 22 of 100 serve callers in a language the AI is not promised to speak. A passed test in even one of Punjabi, Mandarin or Farsi on Ray's own line would open that group.
