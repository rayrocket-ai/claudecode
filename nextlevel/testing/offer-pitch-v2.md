# The pitch each persona hears (offer test, round 2)

This is everything the persona is told. Nothing else may be assumed. Changes from round 1 answer the gaps round 1 found. Every new line is a policy Next Level can keep, not a new capability claim.

**Who:** Ray Ahmadi. GTA real estate broker, Ray Homes Real Estate under eXp Realty, 12+ years, about 50 deals a year, multi-year eXp Icon agent. Works in English and Dari. He runs this system on his own business and shows it live on a call. The service is called Next Level, operated by Ray Ahmadi; the registered business name and address are on the agreement you sign before paying. It is new: the first 10 clients are its founding clients, and there are no client testimonials yet. That is why the founding price exists.

**What (available now): Next Level Answer.** An AI receptionist on your business line. It answers every call you miss or cannot take, says it is an AI, asks your qualifying questions (you choose them: buyer or seller, timeline, pre-approval, rental screening and so on), books the appointment into your calendar, and texts you a summary. If a caller asks for a person or it is urgent, it offers a callback and alerts you by text straight away. You keep your number; calls forward only when you do not answer, including when your phone is off, and forwarding is switched off the day you cancel. Ray's team sets it up for you.

**Founding offer (first 10 clients only):** $300 CAD a month. No setup fee. Price locked while you stay subscribed. Month to month, cancel before the next billing date. Live within 7 days of you supplying what is needed, or the first month is not charged. Feedback calls at day 14 and day 30.

**Usage, in plain terms:** phone, text and AI minutes are billed at cost with no markup for founding clients, on top of the $300. You set a monthly spending cap before go-live and the system stops and alerts you if it is reached. You see Ray's own line's real usage bill on the call before you pay.

**Try before you pay:** on the call you dial the live system yourself and hear it answer as your business would.

**Languages:** English at launch. Any other language is tested on your own line with you during setup. If it does not handle your callers' language to your standard, you do not go live and you are not charged.

**Your callers and your data:** your callers stay yours. No one at Next Level or Ray Homes contacts your callers or uses your contacts, and that is written in the agreement. Calls are transcribed so you get the summary. Audio recording is off unless you turn it on, and when it is on the AI says so at the start of the call. Your contacts and transcripts are exported to you and deleted from the account when you leave.

**Your CRM:** you get every call as a text and an email summary, so it works beside any CRM from day one. A direct push into your CRM is confirmed for your specific CRM on the call before you pay; if it cannot be done, you are told then.

**Your brokerage:** you get a one-page summary to show your broker of record before you sign. If your brokerage says no within 14 days, you get your first month back.

**Teams:** one subscription covers one business line. A team routing calls to several agents is quoted on the call.

**Standard price after the first 10:** $500 setup plus $400 a month.

**Opening next, in this order (not available yet):** Reviews ($250 setup, $150 a month). Nurture ($500, $250 a month). Ads ($500, $750 a month, ad spend on your own card). Complete ($1,500, $1,500 a month): all four.

**For businesses that are not real estate agents:** Answer opens to other businesses once the first 10 agents are live, in this order: mortgage brokers, real estate lawyers, accountants, contractors and renovators, insurance. Starting price: $500 setup plus $400 a month. Join the waitlist and you get first notice and one question now: your industry and your biggest phone problem. Outside those five industries you can still join; no date is promised.

**Where:** Canada first. US clients can join the founding 10 only where setup is confirmed for their state on the call; price is in Canadian dollars.

**How you buy:** a short quiz, then a 15-minute call with Ray (evening and weekend times available), then payment by card.

**Still not stated:** the usage rate per minute as a number; which specific CRMs have a direct push; the registered business name itself.
