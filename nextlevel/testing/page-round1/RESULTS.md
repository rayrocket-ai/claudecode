# Page visitor test, round 1 (2026-11-15)

100 model-written personas walked the page (version 4, bright white, round 3 offer). Finds weak points; not market research, not a forecast.

## What they did

| Outcome | Count |
|---|---|
| Left without starting the quiz | 66 |
| Started the quiz | 34 (all 34 finished) |
| Quiz only | 22 |
| Tried to book | 6 |
| Joined a list | 4 |
| Would buy after the call | 2 |
| Gave contact details | 16 of 34 |

Design score 4.1 of 5. Clarity 3.6 of 5 (phone 3.0, desktop 3.8).

## Where they stopped

Result 34; founding terms 20; first screen 17; method 9; problems 7; final call to action 7.

## Booking fallback (39 reached it)

Fine 10; annoyed 15; gave up 14.

## What hurt

- First screen says neither what it is nor what it costs. "Six questions. One brief..." read as a coaching quiz (5).
- Hero label "For real estate agents" turned away the trades that can now buy (4, plus 8 "not my trade").
- The visible placeholder "[Business name and mailing address to be added]" (6) and no named company (28 mentions).
- Price not seen early (25). Languages not promised (26). Instagram booking fallback (24).
- The form asks for an email though nothing is sent yet (21).
- No way to hear it (14). The founding terms list is where half-readers stop (20).

## What helped

"An AI receptionist answers every call you miss, qualifies the caller and books the appointment."; "$300 a month. No setup fee."; the brokerage one-page summary; recording off; the consent line; the three founding spots line.
