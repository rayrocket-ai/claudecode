# Offer test, round 2 (2026-11-15)

Same 100 model-written personas, pitch `offer-pitch-v2.md`. Finds weak points; not market research, not a forecast.

## Before and after

| Group | Measure | Round 1 | Round 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 70 agents, founding price | Buys | 0 | 3 |
| | Books a call | 7 | 19 |
| | Passes | 63 | 48 |
| 70 agents, standard price | Buys | 0 | 1 |
| | Books a call | 2 | 9 |
| 30 business owners | Joins waitlist | 1 | 5 (one is a competitor) |
| All 100 | Would refer someone | 4 | 11 |
| Agents | Trust in Ray, out of 5 | 2.8 | 2.7 |

## Lines that helped most

- Language tested on your own line, no charge if it is not good enough (7)
- Audio recording off unless you turn it on (10 across two phrasings)
- Dial the live system yourself on the call (6)
- Month to month, cancel before the next billing date (6)
- No one at Next Level or Ray Homes contacts your callers (5)

## Lines that hurt most

- "English at launch." (11)
- "Calls are transcribed so you get the summary." (9): read as a privacy risk without saying who can read the transcript
- "$300 CAD a month." (7): the budget-limited group
- "there are no client testimonials yet" (8 across two phrasings)
- Waitlist starting price of $500 setup plus $400 a month (5): too high for small practices

## Still unanswered (people it mattered to)

Usage rate as a number 23; languages 22; waitlist date 18; US state and USD 18; who can access the data and where it is hosted 13; registered business name 10; a named CRM 9; team pricing 7.

## Reading it

- The fixes moved agents from 7 to 22 who buy or book. Policies that remove risk worked better than descriptions.
- Everything left is a fact only Ray can supply, or proof only founding clients can supply. Wording is close to its limit.
- "Confirmed on the call" earns a booking and almost never a purchase.
- Trust did not move. A named company, a face on the page and the first client results are what move it.
- Small-practice owners (bookkeepers, salons, tax preparers) want something under $100. That is a different product, not this one.
