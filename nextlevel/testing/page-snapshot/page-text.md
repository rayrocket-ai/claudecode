# Next Level sales page: text snapshot

Taken 2026-11-15 from `nextlevel/sales/page/index.html` as rendered in a browser. For the visitor test. Example name used in results: Sam.

## How the page looks and moves

1. The page is a clean, bright true white with near-black text, thin cool-grey rules between sections and a lot of empty space.
2. One vivid colour, an electric blue, does the pointing: the italic phrase in each headline, the buttons, the section numbers, the quiz progress line, the active item in the side index, key prices and the "Available now" tags.
3. One warm colour, a coral orange, is used sparingly: the three missed-call dots and the big "3" in the hero illustration, the small diamonds in the moving strip, and the "Opening next" tags.
4. Headlines are set very large in a high-contrast serif (Bodoni), body text in a plain sans serif, and small labels, buttons and times in spaced-out capitals in a monospace face. Ray's name appears once as a blue handwritten-style signature.
5. Bands alternate: white sections, a pale blue band for the quiz, one deep navy band with a soft blue glow for the Founding 10 price (a huge white "$300"), and a pale blue band for the final call to action.
6. On load the four headline lines rise into place one after another; in the illustration a day line draws from 8 am to 10 pm, three coral dots pop in, the missed-call count ticks up to 3, then a blue panel plays the same 2:14 pm call being answered on a loop (a clock counts up, "Answered. Qualified. Booked." light up in turn, a small waveform moves).
7. As you scroll, sections fade up, rules draw across, the $300 counts up once, a strip of the four problems slides sideways, and on desktop a small numbered index fixed in the left margin marks the section you are in.
8. Buttons fill from the bottom with near-black on hover and lean slightly toward the pointer; quiz answers indent and draw a blue line; quiz screens crossfade and the result unrolls as a white sheet with a blue top edge.
9. Nothing waits on animation to be readable, and with reduced motion switched on nothing moves and the sliding strip becomes plain wrapped text. In a dark-mode browser the same design runs on near-black with lighter blue and coral.
10. On a phone everything is one column: the headline, then the blue "Start the intake" button within the first screen; the side index is hidden; the quiz shows one question per screen with full-width answers; the price table turns into stacked cards; every button and link is at least 44px tall.

### Section 07: Questions

07
Questions
Before you book.
Will callers know it is an AI?

Yes. It says so at the start of the call. If a caller asks for a person, or it is urgent, it offers a callback and alerts you by text straight away.

What does it sound like?

Book the call and dial the live system yourself. You hear it answer before you pay anything.

What is available today?

Next Level Answer is available now. Reviews, Nurture and Ads are opening next, in that order. Complete follows when all four are open.

What do the Founding 10 pay?

$300 a month for Next Level Answer, with no setup fee. The $300 price stays locked while your subscription is active.

What does Answer cost after the Founding 10?

$500 setup and $400 a month, in Canadian dollars.

What does usage cost?

Phone, text and AI minutes are billed at cost with no markup for founding clients, on top of the $300. You set a monthly spending cap before go-live. If it is reached, the system stops and alerts you. On the call Ray shows you the real usage bill for his own line before you pay.

Is there a contract?

No long contract. It is month to month. Cancel before the next billing date. When you leave, your contacts and transcripts are exported to you and deleted from the account.

How long does setup take?

For the Founding 10: live within 7 days of you supplying everything needed, or the first month is not charged. This applies once per client, to the first month only, and not to carrier or registration delays.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. You keep your number. Calls forward only when you do not answer, including when your phone is off. Forwarding is switched off the day you cancel.

What do you need from me?

Your business details, your hours and areas, the questions you want asked, calendar access, the number to forward, and the monthly spending cap you choose.

Does it speak other languages?

English at launch. Any other language is tested on your own line with you during setup. If it does not handle your callers' language to your standard, you do not go live and you are not charged.

I am on another CRM. Does this replace it?

No. It works beside it. You get every call as a text and an email summary from day one. A direct push into your CRM is confirmed for your specific CRM on the call, before you pay. If it cannot be done, you are told then.

Who can see my callers and my call transcripts?

Your callers stay yours. No one at Next Level or Ray Homes contacts your callers or uses your contacts, and that is written in the agreement. Each call is transcribed so you get the summary. The transcript stays in your own account. The people who can read it are you, and the set-up team only while they are setting up or fixing your account. Audio recording is off unless you turn it on.

Will my brokerage allow it?

You get a one-page summary to show your broker of record before you sign. If your brokerage says no within 14 days, you get your first month back.

Is it allowed where I work?

Rules on call recording and on telling callers they are speaking with an AI differ by province and state. We confirm them for your location before you go live. Audio recording is off unless you turn it on, and when it is on the AI says so at the start of the call. Check with your brokerage as well.

Do you have client testimonials?

Not yet. Next Level is new and the first 10 agents are its founding clients. That is the reason the founding price and the founding terms exist. What you can check today is Ray's own track record and the live system, which you dial yourself on the call.

I run a team. How does that work?

One subscription covers one business line. A team routing calls to several agents is quoted on the call.

I am in the US. Can I join?

Canada first. US agents can join the Founding 10 only where setup is confirmed for their state on the call. The price is in Canadian dollars.

Who pays for the ads?

You do, on your own card, straight to the ad platform. The Next Level Ads fee covers running and watching them.

Does this promise me more leads or deals?

No. It makes sure the calls, clients and contacts you already have are answered and followed up.

Do I have to join Ray's team?

No. Next Level is open to agents at any brokerage.

I run a business that is not real estate. Can I use it?

Not yet. Answer opens to other businesses once the first 10 agents are live, in this order: mortgage brokers, real estate lawyers, accountants, contractors and renovators, insurance. The starting price is $500 setup plus $400 a month. Take the intake, pick "I run a different kind of business" and join the waitlist for first notice. Outside those five industries you can still join, and no date is promised.
