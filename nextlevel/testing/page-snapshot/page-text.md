# Next Level sales page: text snapshot

Taken 2026-11-15 from `nextlevel/sales/page/index.html` as rendered in a browser, after the page was brought up to the round 3 offer. For the visitor test. Example name used in results: Sam.

## How the page looks and moves

1. The page is a clean, bright true white with near-black text, thin cool-grey rules between sections and a lot of empty space.
2. One vivid colour, an electric blue, does the pointing: the italic phrase in each headline, the buttons, the section numbers, the quiz progress line, the active item in the side index, key prices and the "Available now" tags.
3. One warm colour, a coral orange, is used sparingly: the three missed-call dots and the big "3" in the hero illustration, the small diamonds in the moving strip, and the "Opening next" tags.
4. Headlines are set very large in a high-contrast serif (Bodoni), body text in a plain sans serif, and small labels, buttons and times in spaced-out capitals in a monospace face. Ray's name appears once as a blue handwritten-style signature.
5. Bands alternate: white sections, a pale blue band for the quiz, one deep navy band with a soft blue glow for the Founding 10 price (a huge white "$300" that stays in view on desktop while 21 short terms scroll beside it), and a pale blue band for the final call to action.
6. On load the four headline lines rise into place one after another; in the illustration a day line draws from 8 am to 10 pm, three coral dots pop in, the missed-call count ticks up to 3, then a blue panel plays the same 2:14 pm call being answered on a loop (a clock counts up, "Answered. Qualified. Booked." light up in turn, a small waveform moves).
7. As you scroll, sections fade up, rules draw across, the $300 counts up once, a strip of the four problems slides sideways, and on desktop a small numbered index fixed in the left margin marks the section you are in.
8. Buttons fill from the bottom with near-black on hover and lean slightly toward the pointer; quiz answers indent and draw a blue line; quiz screens crossfade and the result unrolls as a white sheet with a blue top edge.
9. Nothing waits on animation to be readable, and with reduced motion switched on nothing moves and the sliding strip becomes plain wrapped text. In a dark-mode browser the same design runs on near-black with lighter blue and coral.
10. On a phone everything is one column: the headline, then the blue "Start the intake" button and a short line with a blue rule beside it welcoming mortgage brokers and contractors; the side index is hidden; the quiz shows one question per screen with full-width answers (eight on the first screen); the price table turns into stacked cards; every button and link is at least 44px tall.

## How the quiz routes

The first screen ("Which best describes you?") has eight answers and decides the path.

| First answer | Path | Screens | Ends in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solo agent, Agent with an assistant, Team leader, Real estate broker or brokerage owner | agent | 6 questions, then the contact step | An intake brief on their biggest leak, with Next Level Answer at the founding terms and the booking step |
| Mortgage broker, Contractor or renovator | founding-business | 3 questions, then the contact step | An intake brief worded for their trade, with Next Level Answer at the founding terms and the booking step |
| I run a different kind of business | waitlist | 3 questions (the second asks the industry), then the contact step | A waitlist brief: 1 March 2027 and the starting price for real estate lawyers, accountants and insurance; no date for "Something else". No price to pay today, no booking |
| I am in the United States | us-list | 2 questions, then the contact step | A US list brief. No price, no booking |

On every question: tapping an answer saves it and moves on. Back returns with the earlier answer still selected (shown with a blue line and arrow), and a Next control appears on any question that already has an answer. Before the first answer the progress label reads "Question 1"; after it, "Question 2 of 6" and so on.

On every contact step: if a mobile number is typed, a second unticked box appears: "Yes, you may text me at this number about my result. Reply STOP to end." Neither box is ever ticked for the visitor. Without the email box ticked the result is shown on screen and nothing is sent. Error messages: "Add your first name so the result is written to you." and "Enter a valid email, like name@example.com, or untick the box to see your result here only."
