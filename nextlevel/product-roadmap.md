Draft for Ray to approve. One page. A phase starts when its gate is met, not on a date. The only dates here are ones Ray set or approved. This page holds no revenue projection and no promise of results.

The round 3 offer (`testing/offer-pitch-v3.md`) is the working offer. Approved by Ray, 2026-11-15. Full wording: `offer.md` section 6. The facts under it that are not verified are listed in `README.md` under "Verify before publishing".

## Phase 1: the Founding 10 on Next Level Answer

**What is sold.** Next Level Answer, the AI receptionist, to 10 founding clients. $300 a month, no setup fee, price locked, month to month. The $300 includes 250 AI call minutes, the phone number and up to 500 text segments a month. Extra minutes are $0.30 CAD each, up to a monthly cap the client sets, which can be zero. An extra agent line on the same account is $150 a month and adds 125 minutes to a shared pool. (Approved by Ray, 2026-11-15.)

**Who can buy.** 7 of the 10 founding spots are for real estate agents. 3 are open now to mortgage brokers and to contractors and renovators, on the same founding terms. Founding clients are in Canada only. US agents can join a US list and are told the opening date and the US dollar price by 1 March 2027. (Approved by Ray, 2026-11-15.)

**Target Ray already set.** 10 paying agents by the end of January. With the 3 spots above, that reads as 10 paying founding clients.

**The work.**

- Ray takes every 15-minute call. A demo number is on the page before the call, once Ray supplies it, and the buyer dials the live system again on the call.
- A written agreement that names the registered business goes to the client before payment.
- Every promise in `saas-build-spec.md` section 13 is checked before a client is sold on it.
- Each founder is live within 7 days of supplying what is needed.
- Feedback calls at day 14 and day 30.
- Every sales call fills one row of the real-world scoreboard in `testing/LOOP.md`.

**Gate to leave this phase.** 10 founding clients are live. Onboarding has run the same way each time. Founders have results they agreed in writing to share.

## Phase 4: a licence for others to run it

**What it is.** A white-label software licence. Team leaders, brokerages and resellers run the Next Level snapshot under their own name, on Ray's HighLevel agency. They sell to and support their own clients. Ray supplies the snapshot, the updates and the playbook.

**Who it is for.**

- Team leaders who want it for every agent on their team.
- Brokerages that want it under the brokerage name.
- Resellers who already serve agents or one of the industries in phase 3.

**What must be true before it starts.** All six, with no exceptions.

1. **Repeatable onboarding.** A new account goes live from the checklist by someone other than Ray.
2. **A support process.** A stated way for a licensee and their clients to get help, with who answers and how fast.
3. **A legal entity.** A registered business that signs the licence. Sign-off from Ray's brokerage where it is needed.
4. **Terms.** A licence agreement written with counsel: what the licensee may call it, who owns client data, who is the sender under CASL, what each side must do when a client leaves.
5. **A documented snapshot.** Every workflow, custom value, prompt slot and test written down, so a change made once reaches every licensee safely.
6. **Results from paying clients.** Real numbers from clients who agreed in writing to share them.

**Not decided.** The licence price, the split of setup and monthly fees, and how usage is billed to a licensee: [Ray to set].

## What does not change across the phases

- Each offer runs on Ray Homes before anyone pays for it.
- One product at a time. One industry at a time, apart from the decided exception in phase 3.
- No promise of leads, deals or income.
- Every client's callers and data stay theirs.
