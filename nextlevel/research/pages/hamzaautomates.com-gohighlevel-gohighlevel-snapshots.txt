A GoHighLevel snapshot is a saved copy of one sub-account's setup (funnels, websites, workflows, calendars, pipelines, custom fields, templates, AI agents and more) that you load into any other sub-account in a few clicks. It copies the build and never the data, so contacts, conversations, appointments, phone numbers and integrations like Stripe stay behind.

What is a GoHighLevel snapshot?
HighLevel calls it a reusable template that captures selected configuration assets from a sub-account. You don't build a snapshot directly. You build a sub-account the way you want it, then take a snapshot of that account, and now you can drop the same setup into as many other sub-accounts as you like.
Think of it as the house without the furniture. Every workflow, page, calendar and pipeline comes over, empty and ready for the new business's own leads.

How to create a snapshot
Build the source sub-account first. I keep a clean demo account per niche so nothing from a real client ends up inside a template. Then:
[+]Go to Agency View > Account Snapshots and click + Create New Snapshot.
[+]Name it after the use case (HighLevel's own example is "Spa Funnel Setup") and pick the source sub-account.
[+]Click Select All, or expand each category and tick only what you want.
[+]Click Create. If some assets fail to load, there's a Retry button so you don't have to start over.
How to load a snapshot into a new or existing sub-account
New client: go to Account Snapshots, find the snapshot and click the blue Create New Account (+) button next to it. Fill in the business details and click Add Sub-Account. The snapshot loads as the account is created. You can only pick one snapshot here, and you can load more later.
Existing client: go to Sub-Accounts, click the three dots next to the account and choose Manage Client, then Actions > Load Snapshot. Pick the snapshot, tick the assets you want, and HighLevel shows you any conflicts. Override replaces the client's version with the snapshot's, Skip keeps theirs. Type confirm, click Proceed, and you get an email when it's done.
Be careful with Override. HighLevel says it can't be undone, and a loaded snapshot can't be unloaded either, you'd delete the pieces by hand. Since March 2026 you can also load one snapshot into several sub-accounts at once from Account Snapshots with Load Updates to Sub-Account.
How to share a snapshot (and import one)
Go to Account Snapshots > My Snapshots, click the three dots on the snapshot, choose Share Snapshot, pick a link type and click Get Link. Turn on Assets Protected first if you don't want the other agency reselling your work.
To import one someone sent you, log in to your agency, open their link in a new tab, rename it if you want, and click Import Now. It shows up greyed out under Imported Snapshots until it finishes processing. Importing only adds it to your library. You still have to load it into a sub-account.

How to push snapshot updates to client accounts
Snapshots don't update by themselves. Change something in the source sub-account and the snapshot still has the old version until you refresh it. Go to Account Snapshots, click the Refresh icon on the snapshot, tick the new or changed assets and click Refresh. Every refresh saves a version you can check under the snapshot's Version History tab.
Then click Push Update, pick the sub-accounts that already use the snapshot and which assets to send. HighLevel describes pushes as selective, so you send only what changed. Pushes only reach sub-accounts in your own agency. Another agency has to import a fresh link.
Two controls worth knowing. Edit Snapshot (three dots on the snapshot) lets you switch individual assets and sub-accounts on or off for future pushes. View History shows which accounts succeeded, failed or are still pending. And if a refresh deletes a step in a workflow, HighLevel now removes contacts who were waiting on that step so they don't get stuck.
Snapshots with SaaS mode: auto-load on signup
This is where snapshots really pay off. In the SaaS Configurator, each plan has a Features and Snapshot section. Attach a snapshot to the plan, and when someone pays at checkout, HighLevel creates their sub-account and loads the snapshot on its own. They log in to a finished setup.
Since September 2026, if you turn on SaaS for a sub-account that already has your customizations and attach a snapshot, HighLevel warns you before overwriting anything and lets you keep your versions. Before that it could silently replace edited funnels and websites. You can also attach snapshots you bought on the Marketplace to a SaaS plan.
SaaS mode is on the $497 a month plan (as of October 2026). You don't need SaaS mode to use snapshots. I don't lead with SaaS mode, and I explain why (see SaaS mode guide).

What to check after loading a snapshot
HighLevel's own Hair Salon guide says don't publish pages, turn on workflows, send messages or put live calls through right after installing. Test first. This is the list I'd run through:
[+]Update the custom values (business name, phone, address, booking link) so templates stop saying the placeholder name.
[+]Connect the domain and publish the funnels and websites, they arrive as drafts.
[+]Buy or connect a phone number (see Use your own phone number) and assign it to any Voice AI agent.
[+]Reconnect Stripe, Google, Facebook and anything else, integrations don't copy.
[+]Set calendar availability and staff, then book a test appointment.
[+]Run a test contact through every workflow before switching it on.
[+]Don't load the same snapshot twice into one account. HighLevel warns it can create conflicts or duplicates.

FAQ
Are GoHighLevel snapshots free?+
There's no separate charge for creating, sharing or loading your own snapshots. HighLevel also has free industry snapshots. Snapshots other agencies sell on the Marketplace or their own sites cost whatever they set.
Do snapshots copy contacts?+
No. Snapshots copy configuration only. Contacts, appointments, conversations, message history and reputation data stay in the original account. If you need contacts in the new account, import them with a CSV.
Does a snapshot copy phone numbers and integrations?+
No. Phone numbers, Stripe and other connected accounts like Google and Facebook have to be set up in each sub-account. Voice AI agents copy over, but you assign them a number after the load.
Do snapshot updates push automatically?+
No. You refresh the snapshot after changing the source account, then click Push Update and choose which sub-accounts and assets get it. Pushes only go to sub-accounts in your own agency.
Will loading a snapshot overwrite my client's work?+
Only if you tell it to. When an asset already exists, HighLevel asks you to Override or Skip. Loading doesn't delete contacts or client data, but an override can't be undone, so read the conflict list.
Can I remove a snapshot after loading it?+
Not in one click. HighLevel says a loaded snapshot can't be unloaded. You delete the funnels, workflows and other assets you don't want by hand.
Can I sell my GoHighLevel snapshots?+
Yes. Use Sell on Marketplace from Account Snapshots to list it on HighLevel's App Marketplace as free, one-time, monthly or yearly, or share it yourself with a permanent or one-time link. Turn on Assets Protected so buyers can't resell it.
What's the difference between a snapshot and a template?+
A template is one asset, like a single workflow or email from the template library. A snapshot bundles a whole account's worth of assets, workflows, funnels, calendars, pipelines and fields, so you can copy an entire setup at once.
