Sound familiar?
In most field-service businesses, one person holds the schedule in their head. They spend hours a day building it, then the rest of the day rebuilding it every time a job runs long, a tech calls in sick or a customer cancels. When that person is away, the day falls apart.
✕Someone spends hours a day building and rebuilding the schedule
✕Crews drive back and forth across town between jobs
✕Customers are not home because nobody reminded them
✕The schedule lives in one person’s head
What it does
What the AI Dispatcher handles, around the clock.
1
Builds the schedule
It assigns jobs by skill, location, availability and job length, so the day is planned before anyone gets in.
2
Routes the crews
Jobs are grouped by area to cut drive time between stops.
3
Handles the changes
Sick calls, overruns and cancellations get reshuffled in minutes, not over an hour on the phone.
4
Keeps customers informed
Confirmations, reminders and “on the way” texts go out automatically, so fewer people are not home.
5
Fills gaps
When a slot opens, it offers it to customers on the waitlist or with flexible jobs.
6
Flags problems early
Double bookings, missing parts notes and jobs at risk of running late are raised before they become complaints.

What is an AI dispatcher?
+
It is software that does the dispatcher’s routine work: assigning jobs to the right technician, planning routes, rescheduling when the day changes, and keeping customers updated. Your team still makes the judgement calls. The AI does the shuffling.
Will it work with ServiceTitan, Jobber or Housecall Pro?
+
We connect to the field-service and calendar software you already use wherever it allows it. If your system cannot connect, we tell you on the free consult before any work starts.
Do I still need a dispatcher?
+
Usually you keep the person and lose the busywork. They stop spending hours a day building the board and spend that time on customers, problem jobs and growing the team.
What happens with an emergency job?
+
You set the rules. Emergencies can jump the queue, go to whoever is closest, or go straight to a person for a decision, and the affected customers are notified automatically.
How does an AI dispatcher increase what my business is worth?
+
More jobs per technician and less office time per job raise your margin. And a schedule that runs on a system instead of living in one person’s head removes a key-person risk that buyers mark the price down for.
