Hamza's Story
BUILT ON SYSTEMS. BACKEND BY RESULTS. DESIGNED TO SCALE.
Early Life
Hailing from Karachi, Hamza Baig spent his early years immersed in global cultures—living in Bahrain and Dubai throughout his childhood. Navigating new systems, schools, and societies shaped his ability to adapt, lead, and thrive across borders—an early sign of the global entrepreneur he'd become.
Legacy in Blood 
 Leadership by Design
Hamza Baig's ambition didn't appear by chance—it runs deep in his DNA.
His 16x-great-grandfather was Amir Timur, the legendary 14th-century conqueror who forged an empire spanning Central Asia, Persia, and India. Timur was known not only for his military brilliance, but for his vision of empire-building through strategy, architecture, and legacy. His name lives on as a symbol of relentless expansion and generational power.
Another prominent ancestor, Sir Afsar ul Mulk, served as a Major-General in Hyderabad under British rule. Honored with titles such as K.C.I.E. (Knight Commander of the India Empire) and M.V.O., he exemplified diplomacy and leadership at the highest levels. His service made him a trusted statesman and a decorated figure in both Indian and British history.
Hamza's great-grandfather, Hesky Baig, carried this tradition of excellence into the modern era as an internationally acclaimed polo champion. A companion of Prince Philip, Duke of Edinburgh, Hesky's skill and stature brought him into close circles with the British royal family. The Baig family home still holds historic photos of their time together—testament to a life lived with poise, prestige, and power.
For Hamza, this isn't just family history—it's a call to action. He sees it as a duty to carry the torch forward, not through warfare or politics, but through technology, education, and entrepreneurship.
Hamza Baig is building a new kind of empire—one powered by AI, automation, and leverage. He has helped tens of thousands launch scalable businesses and is pioneering a global movement to redefine what it means to build, lead, and leave a legacy in the digital age.
Cold Beginnings 
 in Canada
In 2017, Hamza Baig, aka Hamza Automates, moved to Canada alone to attend McGill University, a bold leap for someone who had never traveled solo. Leaving the familiarity of home behind, he chose discomfort over ease, knowing growth demanded it.
He pursued a degree in Finance, not out of a love for spreadsheets, but because he believed money was the gateway to freedom. While his peers chased careers, Hamza was chasing leverage. He wasn’t just studying, he was building. From pitch competitions to hackathons and case battles, he honed his voice, sharpened his leadership, and developed the conviction to define his entrepreneurial journey.
Though entrepreneurship was always the endgame, university gave him structure, discipline, and the strategic edge to build faster and better foundations that would later shape Hamza Automates, a brand now trusted by thousands in AI automation.
Building a 
 Global Movement
Today, Hamza Baig is the founder of Hexona Systems—an automation agency and software platform that helps thousands of entrepreneurs and business owners implement AI-powered workflows at scale.
What began as client outreach under the alias "Hamza Automates" quickly became bigger. His content went viral, resonating with a generation hungry to learn automation. His Skool community grew past 45,000 members, and Hamza found himself not just delivering solutions but teaching the world how to build them. This momentum gave birth to the Automation Incubator—his flagship program designed to help others launch their automation agencies and monetize their skills.
Since then, Hamza has spoken on international stages and built a trusted personal brand across YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn, inspiring thousands to embrace AI, systems thinking, and the power of leverage.
