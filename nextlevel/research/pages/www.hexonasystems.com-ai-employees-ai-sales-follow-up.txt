What is an AI SDR or AI appointment setter?
+
It is software that does the first part of the sales job: replying to new leads, asking qualifying questions, following up and booking appointments. A person still closes. The AI makes sure every lead gets a fast reply and nobody falls through the cracks.
Why does speed to lead matter so much?
+
A lead is most interested in the minutes after they ask. If you reply hours later, they have often already called someone else. Replying within a minute, every time, is one of the cheapest ways to win more of the leads you already pay for.
Can it follow up on our old leads and lost estimates?
+
Yes, and it is often where the fastest money is. One solar company had more than 4,000 old leads nobody had contacted. The reactivation campaign booked 38 appointments in under three weeks and produced $24,500 in net profit, with no manual outreach from the sales team.
Will it sound like a robot or annoy my customers?
+
We write every message in your voice, keep it short and personal, and stop as soon as someone says no or asks a person to call. You approve the messages before anything goes live.
Does it replace my sales team?
+
No. It takes the chasing and filtering off them, so they spend their time on people who are ready to buy. Most teams handle far more leads without adding headcount.
How does better follow-up increase what my business is worth?
+
A buyer values a business on its earnings and how dependable they are. Turning more of your existing leads into jobs raises earnings without raising marketing spend, and a sales process that runs on a system, not on one great salesperson, makes those earnings more dependable.
