Where the money goes missing.
Tenants don’t only have problems between nine and five. A leak at 11 PM, a lockout on Saturday, a prospect who wants to see the unit tonight. Most property managers cover this with an after-hours answering service that takes messages, or a manager who never really gets a night off. Neither scales as the portfolio grows.

Is an AI answering service a good fit for property management?
+
Yes, because most calls follow a pattern: maintenance requests, emergencies, showing requests and rent questions. An AI answering service handles those consistently at any hour, creates the work order or booking, and only wakes a person up for a genuine emergency.
How does it decide what counts as an emergency?
+
You set the rules. Typical emergencies are active water leaks, no heat in winter, gas smells, lockouts and anything involving safety. Those go straight to your on-call team with a summary. Everything else becomes a work order for the next business day.
Does it work with AppFolio, Buildium or Yardi?
+
In most cases we connect to the property management software you already use, so work orders and tenant records stay in one place. Where a system has no way to connect, we set up a clean handoff and tell you before we start.
Can it book showings for vacant units?
+
Yes. It answers questions about the unit, checks the prospect against your basic criteria, and books a showing or a self-guided tour slot into your calendar, then sends reminders so fewer people no-show.
How does this make my property management company worth more?
+
Management companies are valued on recurring fee income and on how well they keep owners and tenants. Faster responses help retain management contracts, faster leasing cuts vacancy, and a business that runs after hours without the owner on call is far easier to sell.
