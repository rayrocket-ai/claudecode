What is the GoHighLevel Founder plan?
It's the top self-serve HighLevel plan, at $497 a month. HighLevel's help article puts it simply: "The HighLevel Founder Plan is the new name for the $497/month Agency Pro Plan." It keeps the existing Agency Pro capabilities, everything in the $97 Starter and $297 Unlimited plans, and adds new tools and perks on top, "all of this at the same $497/month subscription price".
HighLevel says it's built for agencies, consultants, service providers and SaaS entrepreneurs. Every plan is compared side by side on Pricing.

Who should get the Founder plan, and who shouldn't
Get Founder when you're actually going to use what's only on it: selling HighLevel as your own software with SaaS mode, or rebilling usage to clients with a markup. That's why I'm on it. Hexona Systems runs as white label software on HighLevel, and HighLevel's SaaSpreneur awards are only for agencies on this plan (more on GoHighLevel awards explained).
If you're just starting an agency, I'd start on Unlimited at $297. You get unlimited sub-accounts and the white label, so clients log in on your domain with your logo, and that's enough to land and serve your first clients. I've also got a whole video on why I don't think leading with SaaS mode works for beginners anymore (see SaaS mode guide). Upgrade when you need what's on Founder.
If you want the perks, the community or the Partner Network, those are Founder only, so that's a real reason to go straight to it.

Already on HighLevel?
If you're on the $497 Agency Pro plan, you're already a Founder. HighLevel's help article says existing Agency Pro customers move over automatically and get the new benefits as they become available, with no action needed.
If you're on Starter or Unlimited, you can upgrade from your agency account: Settings > Billing > Want to Modify/Cancel Subscription? > Upgrade Your Plan, pick monthly or annual, then select the Founder plan and click Upgrade Now.
If you ever downgrade, you lose the features that are only on Founder.

FAQ
How much is the GoHighLevel Founder plan?+
$497 a month, or $4,970 a year paid yearly. It's the same price the Agency Pro plan was. Checked Nov 5, 2026.
Is the Founder plan the same as Agency Pro?+
Yes. HighLevel calls Founder the new name for the $497 Agency Pro plan, with added features and perks at the same price. The differences are on Founder vs SaaS Pro.
Does the Founder plan have a free trial?+
Yes, 14 days on HighLevel's Founder page. Through my link you also get my bonuses.
Do I need the Founder plan for SaaS mode?+
Yes. SaaS mode and rebilling with markup are only on the $497 Founder plan. White label is on the $297 Unlimited plan too.
I'm on Agency Pro. Do I need to do anything?+
No. HighLevel says existing Agency Pro customers move to Founder automatically.
Is the HighLevel Partner Network required?+
No, it's optional. But you need an active Founder plan to be eligible for it.
