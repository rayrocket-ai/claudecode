Will callers know they are talking to an AI?
+
It sounds natural, but we recommend it says it is an AI assistant at the start of the call. Callers care far more about getting an answer and a booking than about who gives it, and being upfront avoids any awkwardness.
What happens with an emergency or a difficult call?
+
You decide the rules. Emergencies, angry customers and high-value jobs can be transferred to a person straight away, with a short summary of the call sent to their phone so they do not have to ask the caller to repeat themselves.
Can it book into the calendar and CRM we already use?
+
In most cases, yes. We connect it to the calendar, CRM or field-service software you already run, so bookings land where your team already looks. If a system has no way to connect, we tell you before we start.
Do I have to change my phone number?
+
No. Calls are forwarded from your existing number, either all the time or only when nobody picks up, so customers keep calling the number they already know.
How is this different from a $49 a month AI receptionist app?
+
An app gives you the tool and leaves the setup, scripts, integrations and tuning to you. We build it into your business, connect it to your follow-up and CRM, run it, and report what it recovered. You are buying the result, not a login.
Is it legal to record and transcribe calls?
+
Yes, with the right notice. Some US states, including California, Florida, Pennsylvania and Washington, require everyone on a call to consent to recording, so the assistant announces it at the start of the call. We set that up for the states you operate in.
