How to automate Google reviews in GoHighLevel, step by step
01
Connect the Google Business Profile
In the client's sub-account go to Settings > Integrations > Google Business Profile > Connect. Sign in with a Google account that has Owner or Manager access, tick the right location and click Connect. The profile has to be verified with Google first. Turn on GBP Call Tracking here too if you want Google calls logged. Connect their Facebook page in the same screen if you want Facebook reviews as well.
02
Fix the profile with GBP Optimization
Reputation > GBP Optimization. It gives the profile a score and flags what's missing: description, photos, categories, services, attributes, hours, phone, social links. Click edit, fill it in, and it updates the live Google profile. It's about 10 to 15 minutes of work.
03
Set the review link
Reputation > Settings > Review Link. Under Get Reviews on Google, pick the connected page. Review Balancing splits traffic between Google and Facebook by a ratio you set (50/50 by default). Custom Link sends everyone to one URL you paste.
04
Turn on SMS review requests
Reputation > Settings > SMS Requests. Flip the toggle top right, set When to send SMS after check-in (Immediately works well), Until clicked, repeat this every, and Maximum retries. Pick the default sender number, then Create New for a template and keep the {{reputation.review_link}} token in it. Set which template sends first and which ones are the retries.
05
Turn on email review requests
Reputation > Settings > Email Requests. Same toggle and cadence (HighLevel's example is 1 hour after check-in), a default sender email, then Set Email Templates to choose the Live template and each Retry template. Drag the Review Link element into the email so the button goes to the right place.
06
Trigger requests from a workflow
Automation > Workflows > Create Workflow. Trigger: Appointment Status with the status filter set to Showed, or Invoice with Invoice Status set to Paid. Action: Send Review Request, then pick SMS or Email (add one of each if you want both). Publish it. Each request uses the sender, timing and retries you set in the two steps above. More on building workflows in GoHighLevel workflows.
07
Turn on Reviews AI
Reputation > Settings > Reviews AI. Pick Suggestive (AI drafts, you click send) or Auto Responses. Click Create Agent or Create Starter Agents, set the tone and prompt, and assign agents by sentiment: positive, neutral, negative. Turn on the option to hold sensitive reviews for a human. If there's a pile of old unanswered reviews, create a Drip Mode campaign to work through them a few a day.
08
Put the reviews on their website
Reputation > Widgets > + Create Widget. Pick a layout (list, grid, carousel, slider and more), the sources and the look, then Save. On a HighLevel site drag the Reviews element onto the page, or click the code icon and Copy Code for any other website. New reviews show up on their own.
How I use reputation management for my own agency
The Google review system in HighLevel is one of the features I like most. You can track your Google reviews, how many you're getting, and how your ranking is doing. I obviously want to move up the rankings as fast as possible, so I send out review requests to my customers, I see the reviews that come in, and my AI responds to them directly from the system.
Then I optimized the profile itself. If you search Hexona Systems you'll see the reviews, the photos, the socials connected. That's because I did what HighLevel's GBP Optimization tab told me to do. Before, my score was 8 out of 16. After, it was 14 out of 16. Things like opening hours, holiday hours and connected social profiles send Google signals that you're still in business and you care about the profile.
Replying to reviews is a big one. It shows people you're active and you care about your customers, and I don't have to do it manually. One thing I don't do myself but would for a client: Google posts. You can schedule them to the Google profile from the Social Planner inside HighLevel.

Which workflow trigger should send the review request?
Use the moment the customer actually got the service. For appointment businesses (med spas, dentists, salons, chiropractors) that's Appointment Status with the filter set to Showed. For trades that invoice after the job (plumbers, roofers, HVAC) it's the Invoice trigger with Invoice Status set to Paid. HighLevel also has a QuickBooks version if the client invoices there.
Keep the filters about the job, never about how the customer felt. Filtering by a tag like "happy customer" or a survey score is exactly the review gating Google bans. More on that below.
Two more ways to collect reviews in person: Sites > QR Codes > Create QR Code with the QR type set to Review Link, for the front desk or the back of an invoice. And Reputation > Requests to send one by hand, which is handy for testing.
Reviews AI: auto replies to Google reviews
Reviews AI writes and posts replies to Google and Facebook reviews. Suggestive mode drafts a reply when you click AI Reply and you send it yourself. Auto Responses lets your Reviews AI Agents reply on their own, with rules by star rating, a wait time before posting, a footer like the business name, and which pages each agent covers.
A setup that works for most clients: a warm, short agent for 4 and 5 stars, and an empathetic agent for negative reviews that apologizes, doesn't argue, and asks the person to call or email so it gets sorted out privately. Turn on the sensitive review hold so the angry ones wait for a human.
If the business has years of unanswered reviews, Drip Mode is the cleanup tool. Reputation > Settings > Reviews AI > Drip Mode > Create a New Campaign, set the date range, replies per day and a time window during business hours, and it works through the backlog without posting 200 replies in one afternoon. Keep it for old reviews and let your normal settings handle new ones.

Review gating, incentives and the FTC rule: what you can't do
This is the part most tutorials skip, and it's the part that can get a client's reviews wiped. Google's Maps policy says a business must not "selectively solicit positive reviews from customers" or discourage negative ones. That's review gating: texting "were you happy?" and only sending the Google link to people who say yes, while unhappy people get a private feedback form. Don't build that, even though it's easy to build in a workflow.
Google also bans incentives of any kind for a review: payment, discounts, free goods or services, or anything in exchange for changing or removing a negative review. So no "10% off if you leave us 5 stars", and no "leave a review to get entered in a draw" either. Owners and staff reviewing their own business is out too.
In the US the FTC's rule on consumer reviews and testimonials has been in effect since October 21, 2024. It bans fake reviews, buying positive or negative reviews, undisclosed reviews from insiders, company-run review sites posing as independent, and review suppression, which includes intimidating people out of posting bad reviews and presenting only your best reviews as if they were all of them. It carries civil penalties.
What that means in HighLevel: send the request to every customer who got the service, with the same link, and let them say what they want. Review Balancing is fine because it splits by percentage, not by mood. The widget's Minimum Ratings filter is fine for a testimonials section, but don't label it "all our reviews". And only text customers who agreed to get texts from the business. This is my read of the rules, not legal advice.
How to sell review automation to a local business
Stop leading with AI. Lead with the easiest thing you can sell, which is fixing their Google Business Profile. Search their trade in your town, scroll past the top three, and find the business with a 3.8 rating, six reviews and no photos. You can spot who needs it in about 10 seconds.
I wouldn't even pitch it as a Google profile. I'd say: I want to help you show up when people ask ChatGPT for a roofer near them, and the reason you're not showing up is your Google profile isn't optimized. Then jump on a 15 to 20 minute screen share, connect their profile together, and show them the score before and after.
From there the review automation is the monthly part. Every finished job gets a request, every review gets a reply, and the widget puts the good ones on their website. You could build this for a local business and charge a monthly fee you set, then add the AI receptionist or missed call text back once they trust you. It's a real business model, and this is how I'd open the door. Both builds are in Build an AI voice agent and Missed call text back.

FAQ
Can GoHighLevel automate Google review requests?+
Yes. Set up SMS and email requests under Reputation > Settings, then add the Send Review Request action to a workflow, for example when an appointment is marked Showed or an invoice is paid. It retries on a schedule you set until the customer clicks the link.
Can GoHighLevel reply to Google reviews automatically?+
Yes, with Reviews AI set to Auto Responses under Reputation > Settings > Reviews AI. Reviews AI Agents reply to Google and Facebook reviews based on star rating and sentiment, and you can hold sensitive reviews for a human to check first.
How much does Reviews AI cost?+
$0.01 per AI reply sent on pay per use, as of October 2026. Generating suggestions is free. It's unlimited, subject to fair use, on the $50 AI Employee Growth or $97 Unlimited plan per sub-account. The $20 Starter plan doesn't include it.
Is review gating allowed in GoHighLevel?+
You can technically build it, but Google's policy bans selectively asking happy customers for reviews, and Google can remove reviews from the profile. Send the same review link to every customer.
Can I offer a discount for a Google review?+
No. Google bans any incentive for a review, including discounts, free services or payment, even if you don't ask for a positive one. In the US the FTC's 2024 rule also bans buying positive or negative reviews.
Do I need Google Business Profile connected?+
To send people to Google and to reply to Google reviews from HighLevel, yes. The profile has to be verified and you need Owner or Manager access. If you only want a custom review page, you can use a Custom Link without connecting it.
Can Reviews AI reply to old reviews?+
Yes. Drip Mode under Reputation > Settings > Reviews AI works through older unanswered reviews on a schedule, with a daily limit and a time window, so they don't all post at once.
How do I show Google reviews on my website?+
Reputation > Widgets > + Create Widget. Choose the layout and sources, save it, then drag the Reviews element onto a HighLevel site or copy the embed code into any other website. It updates on its own as new reviews come in.
