Questions? Answers.
What programs does The Automation Institute offer?
We offer two main programs: The Incubator for beginners covering foundational AI and automation concepts, and The Accelerator for professionals ready to implement sophisticated automations at scale. Both are hosted on Skool with vibrant communities and hands-on learning.
What is the difference between The Incubator and The Accelerator?
The Incubator is designed for beginners new to AI. It covers foundations, essential tools, and core concepts at a self-paced level. The Accelerator is for those ready to dive deep, focusing on advanced systems, scaling strategies, sophisticated automations, exclusive mentorship, and weekly group events.
Do I need coding experience to join?
No. Our programs focus on no-code and low-code tools. The Incubator is specifically designed for beginners with no technical background. You will learn to build powerful automations using visual platforms like Make, Zapier, and GoHighLevel without writing code.
What tools will I learn?
We emphasize practical skills with platforms like OpenAI, Make, GoHighLevel, Airtable, n8n, Zapier, and other no-code tools that enable you to automate marketing, sales, and operations. The curriculum is updated regularly to include the latest AI tools.
What is the Certified Partner program?
Our Certified Partner program recognizes automation agencies and professionals who have completed our training and demonstrated mastery in AI and automation implementation. Partners are listed in our public directory and receive client referrals. You can browse our partners at automationinstitute.ai/partners.
How many students have joined The Automation Institute?
Over 45,000 innovators have joined The Automation Institute community. Our members include professionals, aspiring entrepreneurs, and agency owners from around the world, all leveraging AI and automation to transform their businesses and careers.
Is this suitable for beginners?
Yes. No technical skills are required. Our programs are designed to be accessible to anyone, even those with no coding background, focusing on no-code and low-code solutions. Start with The Incubator to build your foundation.
How do I get started?
If you have questions, start by enrolling in The Incubator. A member of our team will reach out to guide you. For The Accelerator, you can check out securely directly on the Skool Platform. Visit the Programs section on our homepage to compare options.
