How do I get past customers to come back?
+
Remind them at the right time with something useful: a service that is due, a seasonal check-up, or an offer based on what they bought before. Doing that consistently for every customer is the hard part, and it is exactly what the AI handles.
Can it sell maintenance plans or memberships?
+
Yes. It can offer plans at the moments customers are most likely to say yes, such as right after a good job or when a service is due, then handle renewals and reminders automatically.
How does it get more Google reviews?
+
It asks every customer shortly after the job, with a direct link, which is when they are most likely to leave one. Customers who signal a problem are sent to a person first, so you can fix it before it turns into a bad review.
Will customers find it pushy?
+
Not if the messages are useful and well timed. We keep them short and personal, limit how often anyone is contacted, and stop immediately when someone opts out.
How does recurring revenue increase what my business is worth?
+
Recurring revenue is one of the things buyers pay the most for, because it is predictable. A business with a growing base of plan members and repeat customers usually sells for a higher multiple than one that starts from zero every month.
