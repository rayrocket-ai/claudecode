Can AI really write our quotes?
+
It builds them from your own templates, price lists and the notes from the site visit, the same way your office does today, just faster. Anything unusual can be sent to a person to check before it goes out.
Does it work with our CRM and e-signature tools?
+
We connect to the CRM, accounting and document tools you already use wherever they allow it, or set up simple ones if you do not have them. You keep ownership of all of it.
Will this replace our office staff?
+
No. It removes the chasing and copying, so the same team can handle far more jobs. One renovation contractor grew to more than 27 active projects without adding admin staff.
What if a client has questions about a quote?
+
Questions about the quote go to the right person on your team, with the full history attached. The AI handles reminders and routine replies, not negotiations.
How does automating admin increase what my business is worth?
+
Buyers look at output per employee and at how much of the business runs on documented systems. When paperwork runs itself, each person handles more work, margins improve, and the business no longer depends on one office manager who knows where everything is.
