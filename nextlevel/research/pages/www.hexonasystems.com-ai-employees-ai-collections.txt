What it does
What the AI Collections handles, around the clock.
1
Sends invoices on time
Invoices go out the moment a job is complete, not when someone gets to the paperwork.
2
Reminds before the due date
A friendly heads-up with a payment link, so paying on time is the easy option.
3
Chases politely after it
A sequence of firm, courteous follow-ups by text and email, in your tone.
4
Collects deposits
Deposits are requested and confirmed before work is booked, so you are not funding the job.
5
Offers payment plans
Within limits you set, it can offer a split payment instead of letting an invoice go cold.
6
Escalates when needed
Seriously overdue accounts go to a person with the full history, so you only make the calls that matter.

Will automated payment reminders upset my customers?
+
Not when they are written well. Most customers appreciate a friendly reminder with a link to pay. You approve the tone and timing, and anyone who replies with a question or a problem is handed to a person.
Does it work with QuickBooks or Xero?
+
We connect to the accounting and invoicing software you already use wherever it allows it, so reminders stop the moment an invoice is marked paid.
Can it collect deposits before a job is booked?
+
Yes. It can request a deposit as part of booking and only confirm the appointment once it is paid, using rules you set for each type of job.
Is this a debt collection agency?
+
No. It is your own polite, consistent follow-up on your own invoices, sent in your business’s name. Accounts that need formal collection are flagged to you to decide what to do.
How does faster collection increase what my business is worth?
+
Buyers look closely at cash flow and working capital. A business that gets paid quickly and predictably needs less cash tied up to run, which makes it less risky to buy and easier to finance.
