## 2. The five lessons to take next

Ranked by what Next Level is missing. Each one is something the notes say exists and was not watched, or was watched without a transcript.

**1. The four videos linked under the pre-built automations.**
Why: the notes' biggest gap is building. These are the only free material that may show a build on screen. Watch for: the voice receptionist flow, speed-to-lead, and how each one is tested. Fill one lesson note per video using `course-notes-template.md`.

**2. The missed-call text-back calculator video (8 minutes).**
Why: "$300 a month is a lot" is the most likely objection, and the call script deliberately lets the agent do their own math. Watch for: which inputs the calculator asks for, so Ray can ask the same questions aloud. Use the prospect's own numbers only. Do not carry any example figure or projected return from the video into Next Level copy.

**3. The inbound content lesson, again, with the transcript downloaded.**
Why: inbound is Ray's main lead source and the notes hold only the outline. Watch for: how the profile is laid out, the exact parts of the lead magnet post, how the comment turns into a conversation, and what the form asks for. The transcript is for Ray's own study and stays out of this folder.

**4. The builder tour and the platform tour, again, with the transcripts.**
Why: `saas-build-spec.md` still has many `[verify in HighLevel]` marks. Watch for: AI agent settings, what a snapshot carries, enrollment history and execution logs, and re-entry settings. Diana should watch with the spec open and replace each mark with yes, no or how.

**5. The partnerships lesson, and the private advanced sales course if access is offered at no cost.**
Why: partners who already serve agents are the one channel in the notes that needs no cold contact and fits a working broker's network. Watch for: how the first conversation with a partner is opened, what proof a partner asks for, and how terms are written down. If the advanced course opens, watch the discovery and closing lessons first.
