Draft for Ray to approve. 14 short scripts (2 a day for 7 days), 2 long-form YouTube outlines, 3 carousels.

Every piece goes to Slack #agent-marketing for Ray's check mark before it posts. Perspective from a working broker, not legal or financial advice.

## Before filming: rules for every piece

- One call to action per piece. It is the keyword and nothing else.
- No real caller's name or phone number on screen. When Ray plays a caller he uses a test name. Any log or dashboard is Ray's own line with caller details hidden or cropped.
- Leave in the moment the receptionist says it is an AI assistant.
- No promise of leads, deals or income. No guarantee.
- Where a script says [Ray to add: real number from Ray Homes], Ray either says the real number or skips the line. Each script is written to work as a live demonstration without it.
- Recording and publishing a call with the receptionist: [Ray to confirm with counsel: recording a call with the AI receptionist for content, and showing it publicly]
- Spoken length: 45 to 75 seconds. If a take runs long, cut beat 2 first.
- Lines where Ray describes how he built or uses his own system (S03, S06, S07, S12, S13) are written for Ray to confirm as true, or reword, before filming. Nothing about Ray's history is to be said on camera unless it is true.

I am a working broker and I sell an AI receptionist to other agents. So I am going to do something odd for a sales video. I am going to tell you who should not buy one, what it cannot do, and what to check before you pay anyone, including me.

**Beat 1: The problem in one minute**
You cannot be in a showing and on the phone. Voicemail asks nothing and books nothing. No statistics. Ray's own experience only.

**Beat 2: What it does**
Answers, says it is an AI, asks the qualifying questions, books into the calendar, sends a summary, texts back a missed call. Show each on screen with a test call.

**Beat 3: What it cannot do**
It does not price a home. It does not negotiate. It does not give advice. It does not replace the relationship. It hands off to a person when asked.

An agent whose calls are always answered by a person. An agent whose phone is not ringing yet. Say it plainly.

**Beat 5: The questions to ask any provider**
Does it tell callers it is an AI? Who owns the contacts? Can I leave, and do I get my data? Are recording and AI disclosure rules confirmed for my province or state?
