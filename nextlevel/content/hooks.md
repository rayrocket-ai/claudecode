# Next Level hooks

Draft for Ray to approve. 60 hooks in Ray's voice, grouped by pillar. Each is the first line Ray says on camera, and the first line of the caption.

How a hook is built here: authority (a working broker), curiosity (what they have not seen), stake (what it costs them). One hook per piece. Every hook must be paid off in the piece.

Rules that apply to every line: no income or results claims, no guarantees, no invented numbers. The only facts about Ray stated are that he is a GTA broker with Ray Homes Real Estate under eXp Realty, 12+ years, about 50 deals a year, a multi-year eXp Icon agent, and that he speaks English and Dari. Where a hook needs a real number from Ray's system, it is marked. Perspective, not legal or financial advice.

Audience tags: **A** = agent, **B** = business owner.

Ten hooks also carry a Dari line. Each is marked **Ray to review Dari**.
