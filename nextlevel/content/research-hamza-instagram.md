## What we take

These are mechanics and structures. No scripts, captions, titles, slogans, lead magnet names or visual style are copied.

1. **One keyword per video, tied to what was shown.** The comment is the lead. It also tells us which problem the person has.
2. **One industry per piece.** Ray starts with one: real estate agents. The business lane later gets one industry per piece too.
3. **Show the system working.** Screen and phone on camera, not only Ray talking.
4. **A free resource that is the thing in the video.** If the video shows the call script, the resource is the call script.
5. **Short clips point to a longer lesson.** One long YouTube video a week, cut into shorts.
6. **Same piece on every platform, one link hub.**
7. **Consistency over targets.** Teach one useful thing a day and let it compound. This fits one filming session a day.

## What we do differently

| His model (as far as the sources show) | Ray's version |
|---|---|
| Audience is mostly people who want to start an automation business. | Audience is the business owner who has the problem. Agents first. The buyer and the viewer are the same person. |
| Hooks lean on dollar figures attached to a build. | No income, earnings or results claims. The hook is the stake the agent already feels: the call that went to voicemail during a showing. |
| Teaches the build so viewers can copy it. | Shows the result so viewers can judge it. Ray is not selling a course. He sets it up and runs it. |
| Many industries from day one. | One niche first. Other industries open one at a time, in the order in `offer.md`, and until then they join a waitlist. |
| Free community as the middle step. | No community to run. The middle step is a 6-question quiz and a free missed-call audit, then a 15-minute call where Ray dials the receptionist live. |
| Creator and teacher. | A working broker who uses the system on his own business. 12+ years, about 50 deals a year, multi-year eXp Icon agent. The content is filmed inside a real practice. |
| English. | English and Dari. The newcomer market is served in its own language. Dari lines are reviewed by Ray before they post. |
| Recorded walkthroughs. | Live demos on a real phone line. Ray calls the receptionist on speaker on camera. No caller's name or number is ever shown. |
| Keyword replies sent by automation. | Replies are drafted by an AI and sent by Ray or Diana by hand until DM automation is connected and approved. |

## What a human should check on Instagram (30 minutes)

1. The current bio line and the link in bio.
2. The last 30 posts: how many are talking head, screen recording, flowchart, interview.
3. The first sentence of the top 10 reels by views, written down as structures, not copied.
4. Whether the keyword call to action is spoken, on screen, in the caption, or all three.
5. How often he posts in a normal week.
6. Whether a comment gets an instant DM and what the DM asks for.
7. The pinned posts.

Then update rows 1, 4, 8, 12 and 14 above and change their confidence.
