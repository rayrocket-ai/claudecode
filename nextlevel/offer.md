Draft for Ray to review. Nothing here is public until Ray approves it.

## 1. Who it is for

### Agent edition (now)

Working real estate agents and small teams in Canada and the US who:

- take their own calls and miss some of them during showings, listing appointments and evenings,
- have a database they rarely contact,
- ask for reviews when they remember,
- run ads, or want to, and do not watch them daily.

Sold by a working GTA broker who runs the same systems on his own business.

### Business edition (after the gate)

Ray wants an offer for businesses, not only agents. The offer is built for that from day one: one master snapshot, two editions. The agent edition sells now. The business edition is the same snapshot with the prompt, stage names, custom values and review links swapped for the industry.

**What the gate allows today**

- Design the snapshot so the swap is a settings change, not a rebuild. (Covered in `saas-build-spec.md`.)
- Write the industry versions of the prompt and stage names as drafts.
- Keep a waitlist of business owners who ask. They must opt in themselves (CASL).

**What waits for the gate**

- Any sale, checkout link or price for a non-agent business.
- Any ad, page or outbound message aimed at a non-agent industry.
- Onboarding a non-agent business, paid or free.

The gate, from the Blueprint: each new industry starts only when real estate has paying clients and a repeatable onboarding. Industries open one at a time, in this order.

| Order | Industry | First offer (PROPOSED, Ray to approve) | Why this one first |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mortgage brokers | AI receptionist | Their callers are the same people Ray's callers are, and a missed call goes to the next broker. Closest to what is already proven. |
| 2 | Real estate lawyers | AI receptionist, set to intake only | Closing calls arrive in bursts. The agent books and collects details. It gives no legal information. |
| 3 | Accountants | Reviews | Work is seasonal and referral-led. Review requests at filing are the simplest fit. |
| 4 | Contractors and renovation | AI receptionist | Owners are on job sites and cannot pick up. |

## 2. The problem, in the buyer's words

Use their words on the page and on the call. These come from the four problems in the Blueprint.

**Missed calls**
- "I was in a showing. By the time I called back they had booked with someone else."
- "My voicemail is full of people I never reached."

**Reviews**
- "My clients are happy. I forget to ask."
- "The agent down the street has more Google reviews than I do and I do more deals."

**Dead database**
- "I have years of contacts sitting there. I have not talked to most of them."
- "I know there is business in my list. I do not have the hours."

**Ads**
- "I boosted a listing and have no idea what it did."
- "Leads came in. Nobody called them fast enough."

## 5. What is included in setup

For Next Level Answer (the only rung on sale):

1. A HighLevel sub-account cloned from the Next Level snapshot.

3. The voice agent set up with the agent's name, areas, hours, services and common questions.

6. A pipeline so every caller is a contact with a stage.

## 11. Objections, with honest answers

**"My callers will hate talking to an AI."**
Some will. The agent says at the start that it is an AI assistant, and any caller can ask for a person. The comparison is not AI against you. It is AI against voicemail. Call the demo line and judge it yourself. [Ray to add: real number from Ray Homes]

**"I already have voicemail, or an assistant."**
Voicemail does not qualify or book. An assistant does, during the hours they work. This covers the rest: showings, evenings, weekends.

**"$300 a month is a lot."**
It is. The honest test is your own missed calls. The free audit shows what happened when we called your line. If you rarely miss a call, you do not need this, and we will say so.

**"Is this legal? Recording calls, AI on the phone?"**
The rules on recording and on telling callers they are speaking with an AI differ by province and state. We confirm them for your location before you go live. [Ray to confirm with counsel]

**"Will it work with my CRM?"**
It runs in its own HighLevel account that we set up for you. What connects to your current CRM: [Ray to confirm: which CRMs, if any, are supported at launch].

**"What if I want out?"**
PROPOSED, Ray to approve: month to month, cancel before the next billing date. Your contacts are yours and we export them for you.

**"You are a broker. Are you going to take my leads?"**
No. Your sub-account is yours. Ray's team does not work your contacts. [Ray to confirm: put this in the client agreement]

**"Do you have results from other agents?"**
Not yet. The Founding 10 are the first. What we have is Ray's own business: [Ray to add: real number from Ray Homes]. That is why the founding price is what it is.

**"I am not an agent. Can I buy it?"**
Not yet. The business edition opens one industry at a time, mortgage brokers first. Join the waitlist and we will contact you when your industry opens.
