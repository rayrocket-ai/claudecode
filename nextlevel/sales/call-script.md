# The 15-minute call

Draft for Ray to approve. Ray is the seller and the closer on every call for now. Nadiya later. This is the one human step in the sales machine. Everything before and after it runs on its own.

Builds on Ray's 5-step pitch script. Steps here: open, discover, demo, offer, close.

## Before the call (2 minutes)

- Open the contact. Read the quiz result: missed calls, reviews, dead database or ads.
- Note where they came from: which platform and which piece of content. Most calls are inbound.
- Read the audit log line if an audit was done (optional tactic).
- Have the demo line ready to dial on speaker. [Ray to add: demo line number]
- Have the checkout link ready. Blocked until Stripe verification. Until then: take the yes, book the onboarding call, send the link when it is live.
- Know how many founding places are left. Say the true number.

## 1. Opening

"Hi {{first name}}, Ray here. Thank you for booking.

A quick word on who I am. I am a real estate broker in the GTA, Ray Homes Real Estate under eXp Realty. 12+ years, about 50 deals a year. Everything I show you today runs on my own business.

We have 15 minutes. I will ask you a few questions about how calls and leads work in your business today. Then I will call my AI receptionist live so you can hear it. Then I will tell you plainly if it fits you. If it does, we will get you started today. Sound good?"

Wait for the yes.

"Your quiz said your biggest leak is {{problem}}. Tell me about that."

## 2. Five discovery questions

Ask, then stop talking. Write down their exact words.

1. **"Walk me through what happens today when someone calls and you are with a client."**
   Listening for: voicemail, a spouse, an assistant with set hours, nothing.

2. **"How many calls a week come in from people you do not know yet? Your best guess."**
   Listening for: their number, not ours. Use it later.

3. **"When you miss one, how long before you call back? And how often are they still looking?"**
   Listening for: the cost in their words.

4. **"What have you already tried to fix it?"**
   Listening for: answering service, assistant, another tool. Why it did not hold.

5. **"If every call was answered and booked for the next 30 days, what would be different for you?"**
   Listening for: the result they want. Repeat it back in their words.

Bridge:
"So today {{their words from Q1}}. You get about {{their number}} new calls a week and some of them are gone by the time you call back. And what you want is {{their words from Q5}}. Did I get that right?"

If the call shows they rarely miss calls, say so: "From what you have told me, this is not your problem. I would not spend $300 a month on it." Then point them to the community and end well.

## 3. The demo moment

"Let me show you instead of telling you. I am going to call my receptionist now. Listen to how it opens, what it asks, and how it books."

Put the phone on speaker. Dial the demo line.

Play a caller that matches their world. For example: a buyer who saw a listing and wants to see it this weekend.

Let the agent hear:
- the greeting and the line that says it is an AI assistant,
- two or three qualifying questions,
- the booking,
- the goodbye.

Hang up. Then be quiet for a moment.

"What did you notice?"

Let them talk. Then:

"After that call I get a text with a summary and the appointment is on my calendar. I did nothing."

Optional, if they are keen: "Want to call it yourself? Here is the number."

Proof, in this order:
1. The track record from the opening. The only facts to state: GTA real estate broker, Ray Homes Real Estate under eXp Realty, 12+ years, about 50 deals a year, multi-year eXp Icon agent, bilingual in English and Dari.
2. The live demo they heard a moment ago.
3. Ray's own system numbers, shown live: "Here is my own account at Ray Homes. These are my calls and my bookings." Share the screen or read from the dashboard. If a figure is said aloud, it is real and current: [Ray to add: real number from Ray Homes].

If the demo fails on the call: say so plainly. "That is not how it should go. I will find out why and send you a recording of a clean call today." Do not sell past a bad demo.

## 4. The offer

"Here is what I am doing. I am taking 10 agents first. I call them the Founding 10.

You get the same receptionist, set up for your business: your name, your areas, your questions, your calendar. It answers every call you do not pick up. It qualifies and books. If someone hangs up, it texts them back. You get a summary of every call.

We set all of it up for you. You are live in 7 days from the day you send us your details. We check in on day 3 and day 14, and you get a results report every month."

## 5. The price

"The founding price is $300 a month."

Stop. Let them answer.

Then, only what Ray has approved:
- [Ray to set: usage included or rebilled for the Founding 10]
- [Ray to set: contract terms for the Founding 10]

## 6. The two most likely objections

### "My callers will not want to talk to an AI."

"That is a fair worry. A few will not. It tells them at the start that it is an AI assistant, and anyone who asks for you gets passed to you.

Here is the real comparison. Today those callers get your voicemail. You heard the call a minute ago. Which one would you rather a new buyer reach at 8 PM?"

If they are still unsure: "Call the demo line yourself tonight. Try to trip it up. Then decide."

### "$300 a month. I need to think about it."

"Of course. What part do you want to think through?"

Listen. It is usually one of three:

- **Is it worth it?** "You told me you get about {{their number}} new calls a week and you miss some. I cannot promise you what those calls are worth. You know your business. What is one answered call worth to you?" Let them do the math. Do not do it for them.
- **Will it work for me?** "That is what the first 14 days are for. We listen to your real calls with you on day 3 and day 14 and fix what is off." If Ray approves the live-in-7-days commitment in `offer.md`, state its exact terms here.
- **I need to ask my broker or partner.** "Good idea. Shall we book a short call with them on the line?" Book it before hanging up.

Do not push. Do not invent a deadline. If the founding places are nearly gone, say the true number.

## 7. The close

"Based on what you told me, this fits. You miss calls when you are with clients and you want {{their words from Q5}}. Let's get you set up.

I will send the checkout link to your phone now. Once that is done you get a short form. Send it back today and your 7 days start today. Which email do you want the form sent to?"

If yes:
1. Send the checkout link. Stay on the line while they complete it.
2. Book the onboarding call before hanging up.
3. Move the stage to Won.

If not today:
1. Agree one clear next step with a date and time. Book it on the calendar now.
2. Send the demo line number so they can test it.
3. Log their exact objection in the contact.

If it is a no:
"Understood. Thank you for the time." Offer the community. Mark the stage. No more sales emails unless they asked for them.

## 8. What happens in the next 7 days

Say this at the end of every yes.

"Here is your next 7 days.

- **Today:** you pay and get the intake form. It takes a few minutes. It asks for your hours, areas, questions and the number to forward.
- **When the form is back:** we build your account from our template and set up your receptionist.
- **Next:** we connect your phone and your calendar.
- **Then:** we test it ourselves. After that you call it and tell us what to change.
- **By day 7:** you are live. Calls you do not pick up are answered and booked.
- **Day 3 after go-live:** we listen to your first real calls and tune it.
- **Day 14:** we review your numbers together.

Before you go live we confirm the call recording and AI disclosure rules for your province or state. [Ray to confirm with counsel]

The one thing I need from you is the form, today. We will talk on {{onboarding call date}}."
