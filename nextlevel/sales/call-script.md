# The 15-minute call

Draft for Ray to approve. Ray is the seller and the closer on every call for now. Nadiya later. This is the one human step in the sales machine. Everything before and after it runs on its own.

Builds on Ray's 5-step pitch script. Steps here: open, discover, demo, offer, close.

**Before this call ever happens.** Every first contact (a comment reply, a direct message, a hello at an event) has one goal: this 15-minute call. No price and no pitch before it. (Added from the course notes, 2026-11-15.)

## Before the call (2 minutes)

- Open the contact. Read the quiz result: missed calls, reviews, dead database or ads.
- Note where they came from: which platform and which piece of content. Most calls are inbound.
- Read the audit log line if an audit was done (optional tactic).
- Have the live line ready for them to dial themselves. [Ray to add: demo line number]
- Have the real usage bill for your own line open, ready to show before they pay. (Set 2026-11-15 after the persona test; Ray to confirm.)
- Have the one-page brokerage summary ready to send.
- Check the quiz and booking notes for their CRM, their callers' languages, whether they run a team, and their province or state. Each has a set answer in section 5.
- Have the checkout link ready. Blocked until Stripe verification. Until then: take the yes, book the onboarding call, send the link when it is live.
- Know how many founding places are left. Say the true number.
- Read the pre-call brief if there is one: three lines drafted by the Leads and Booking role from the quiz answers and the content they came from. It is a draft to read, not a script to follow. (Added from the course notes, 2026-11-15. See `learning/apply-map.md`.)

## 1. Opening

"Hi {{first name}}, Ray here. Thank you for booking.

A quick word on who I am. I am a real estate broker in the GTA, Ray Homes Real Estate under eXp Realty. 12+ years, about 50 deals a year. Everything I show you today runs on my own business.

We have 15 minutes. I will ask you a few questions about how calls and leads work in your business today. Then you will dial my AI receptionist yourself and hear it answer. Then I will tell you plainly if it fits you. If it does, we will get you started today. Sound good?"

Wait for the yes.

"Your quiz said your biggest leak is {{problem}}. Tell me about that."

## 2. Five discovery questions

Ask, then stop talking. Write down their exact words.

1. **"Walk me through what happens today when someone calls and you are with a client."**
   Listening for: voicemail, a spouse, an assistant with set hours, nothing.

2. **"How many calls a week come in from people you do not know yet? Your best guess."**
   Listening for: their number, not ours. Use it later.

3. **"When you miss one, how long before you call back? And how often are they still looking?"**
   Listening for: the cost in their words.

4. **"What have you already tried to fix it?"**
   Listening for: answering service, assistant, another tool. Why it did not hold.

5. **"If every call was answered and booked for the next 30 days, what would be different for you?"**
   Listening for: the result they want. Repeat it back in their words.

**What to listen for beyond the phone.** (Added from the course notes, 2026-11-15.) Each of these points to a later rung. Write it down. Do not sell it on this call.

| They say | It points to |
|---|---|
| Web or ad leads wait hours for a reply | Answer (speed-to-lead), later Ads |
| Calls after hours and on weekends go nowhere | Answer |
| Few Google reviews, or they forget to ask | Reviews |
| Contacts are in a phone, a spreadsheet or nowhere | Nurture |
| Nobody watches the ads | Ads |

One extra question when they came from a form or an ad, not a phone problem:
"What happens when someone fills in the form on your website at 10 PM on a Friday?"

Bridge:
"So today {{their words from Q1}}. You get about {{their number}} new calls a week and some of them are gone by the time you call back. And what you want is {{their words from Q5}}. Did I get that right?"

If the call shows they rarely miss calls, say so: "From what you have told me, this is not your problem. I would not spend $300 a month on it." Then point them to the community and end well.

## 3. The demo moment

"Let me show you instead of telling you. Here is the number. Dial it yourself now, the way one of your callers would. Listen to how it opens, what it asks, and how it books."

They dial the live system themselves. (Set 2026-11-15 after the persona test; Ray to confirm.) Give them the number: [Ray to add: demo line number]

Suggest a caller that matches their world. For example: a buyer who saw a listing and wants to see it this weekend. If they would rather listen, dial it on speaker and play that caller.

Let the agent hear:
- the greeting and the line that says it is an AI assistant,
- two or three qualifying questions,
- the booking,
- the goodbye.

Hang up. Then be quiet for a moment.

"What did you notice?"

Let them talk. Then:

"After that call I get the summary by text and by email, and the appointment is on my calendar. I did nothing."

Proof, in this order:
1. The track record from the opening. The only facts to state: GTA real estate broker, Ray Homes Real Estate under eXp Realty, 12+ years, about 50 deals a year, multi-year eXp Icon agent, bilingual in English and Dari.
2. The live demo they heard a moment ago.
3. Ray's own system numbers, shown live: "Here is my own account at Ray Homes. These are my calls and my bookings." Share the screen or read from the dashboard. If a figure is said aloud, it is real and current: [Ray to add: real number from Ray Homes].

If the demo fails on the call: say so plainly. "That is not how it should go. I will find out why and send you a recording of a clean call today." Do not sell past a bad demo.

## 4. The offer

"Here is what I am doing. I am taking 10 agents first. I call them the Founding 10.

You get the same receptionist, set up for your business: your name, your areas, your questions, your calendar. It answers every call you do not pick up. It qualifies and books. If someone hangs up, it texts them back. You get a summary of every call by text and by email. If a caller asks for a person, or it is urgent, it offers a callback and texts you straight away. You keep your number. Calls forward only when you do not answer.

We set all of it up for you. You are live in 7 days from the day you send us your details. We check in on day 3 and day 14, and you get a results report every month."

In the offer above, the summary by text and email, the callback and text alert, and the forwarding rule: Set 2026-11-15 after the persona test; Ray to confirm.

One-line version, if they ask what it is in a sentence. PROPOSED, Ray to approve: "Your calls are answered and booked without you stepping out of a showing."

## 6. The two most likely objections

### "My callers will not want to talk to an AI."

"That is a fair worry. A few will not. It tells them at the start that it is an AI assistant. If someone asks for you, or it is urgent, it offers a callback and texts you straight away.

Here is the real comparison. Today those callers get your voicemail. You heard the call a minute ago. Which one would you rather a new buyer reach at 8 PM?"

If they are still unsure: "Call the demo line yourself tonight. Try to trip it up. Then decide."

### "$300 a month. I need to think about it."

"Of course. What part do you want to think through?"

Listen. It is usually one of three:

- **Is it worth it?** "You told me you get about {{their number}} new calls a week and you miss some. I cannot promise you what those calls are worth. You know your business. What is one answered call worth to you?" Let them do the math. Do not do it for them.
- **Will it work for me?** "That is what the first 14 days are for. We listen to your real calls with you on day 3 and day 14 and fix what is off." Then state the live-in-7-days commitment in its exact terms (Approved by Ray, 2026-11-15): "If you are not live within 7 days of sending us everything we need, your first month is not charged. That applies once, to the first month, and not when the delay is a carrier or registration approval outside our control."
- **I need to ask my broker or partner.** "Good idea. I will send you a one-page summary to show your broker of record before you sign. If your brokerage says no within 14 days, you get your first month back. Shall we also book a short call with them on the line?" Send the summary and book the call before hanging up. (Set 2026-11-15 after the persona test; Ray to confirm.)

Do not push. Do not invent a deadline. If the founding places are nearly gone, say the true number.

### Three more, short answers

- **"I do not have time to set this up."** "You do not set it up. We do. Your part is one short form and one test call to your own line."
- **"AI does not apply to my business."** "You do not have to use AI anywhere else. This does one job: it picks up the calls you cannot take and books them. You heard it a minute ago."
- **"Next Level is new."** "It is. There are no client testimonials yet, and that is the reason the founding price and the founding terms exist. My business is not new. I have worked in GTA real estate for 12+ years and this runs on my own line today. That is why I showed it to you live."

## 7. The close

"Based on what you told me, this fits. You miss calls when you are with clients and you want {{their words from Q5}}. Let's get you set up.

I will send the checkout link to your phone now. Once that is done you get a short form. Send it back today and your 7 days start today. Which email do you want the form sent to?"

If yes:
1. Send the checkout link. Stay on the line while they complete it.
2. Book the onboarding call before hanging up.
3. Move the stage to Won.

If not today:
1. Agree one clear next step with a date and time. Book it on the calendar now.
2. Send the demo line number so they can test it again. Send the one-page brokerage summary if they need their broker's yes.
3. Log their exact objection in the contact.
4. Ask: "Shall I email you a one-page summary of what we covered?" If yes, send the after-call summary the same day (`follow-up-emails.md`, "After-call summary"). Log the yes. (Added from the course notes, 2026-11-15.)

If it is a no:
"Understood. Thank you for the time." Offer the community. Mark the stage. No more sales emails unless they asked for them.

## 8. What happens in the next 7 days

Say this at the end of every yes.

"Here is your next 7 days.

- **Today:** you pay and get the intake form. It takes a few minutes. It asks for your hours, areas, questions, the number to forward and the monthly spending cap you choose.
- **When the form is back:** we build your account from our template and set up your receptionist.
- **Next:** we connect your phone and your calendar.
- **Then:** we test it ourselves. After that you call it and tell us what to change. If your callers use a language other than English, we test that on your own line with you. If it is not good enough for you, you do not go live and you are not charged.
- **By day 7:** you are live. Calls you do not pick up are answered and booked.
- **Day 3 after go-live:** we listen to your first real calls and tune it.
- **Day 14:** we review your numbers together.
- **Day 30:** a short feedback call.

Before you go live we confirm the call recording and AI disclosure rules for your province or state. [Ray to confirm with counsel]

The one thing I need from you is the form, today. We will talk on {{onboarding call date}}."
