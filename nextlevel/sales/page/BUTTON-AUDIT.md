## What this rerun covered

- **Page controls**, at all five widths in both themes: every link and button outside the intake, including the two new expanders ("All 21 terms", "All 22 questions"), the "See a sample call" button and the Close control inside its panel, the hero link "Join the US list", the "I am in the United States" control inside the folded terms, all 22 FAQ rows, and the side index at 1280 and 1440px.
- **The expanders**: six rows shown first and the rest folded; the folded rows are in the DOM with `content-visibility: hidden`; the button carries `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`, opens and closes by mouse, Enter and Space; a find-in-page match inside a folded list opens it (`beforematch`); print shows the folded rows while closed.
- **The sample call panel**: opens as a modal dialog with focus inside, Tab stays inside, Esc, the Close control and a click on the backdrop each close it and return focus to the opener; it fits the viewport at every width; it is labelled "Sample script for illustration, not a recording" and holds the AI disclosure, two qualifying questions, the booking and the text summary, with no phone number and no address.
- **Six walks of the intake in both modes**, at every width in both themes. Every answer on every screen was pressed by mouse and by Enter, with Back checked after each; Space, Back and Next were checked on every screen; the last step and every control in the result were used.
  - `WEBHOOK_URL` empty (the page as published): the last step asks only for an optional first name, has no email, mobile or consent field, the button reads "Show my brief", the result says "This brief is shown here only. Nothing was sent or stored.", and **zero requests** left the page across all six walks.
  - `WEBHOOK_URL` set (stubbed endpoint): the earlier form is unchanged; name is required; a bad email is refused when consent is ticked; no box is ever pre-ticked; the text box appears only with a mobile number; without consent the result is shown and nothing is sent; with consent **exactly one POST** per walk, with the same 16 payload fields in the same order and the right path, industry and tags. The payloads are printed at the end of this file.
- **The booking fallback** (`BOOKING_URL` empty): the buying results show "Ask Ray for a time" with one button, "Message NEXT on Instagram" (copies NEXT, opens the profile in a new tab, confirms on the page), the line "The online calendar opens shortly." and no control that looks like a calendar button. "Ray will contact you at the details you gave." appears beside it only when the webhook is set and consent was ticked.
- **Settings variants**: `BOOKING_URL` set (single booking button as before, in the last section and in the result, no fallback wording); `BOOKING_URL` and `WEBHOOK_URL` both set; `DEMO_PHONE` empty and set.
- **Result wording by rule**: the two trade results, their questions and last step contain no "showing", "listing", "buyer", "seller" or singular "agent"; the waitlist and US list results carry no founding price and no booking step; no result mentions a demo number or a guarantee.
- **First screen**: at 390 x 844 and 360 x 740 (phone emulation), with and without reduced motion, and at 768, 1280 and 1440px wide, the headline, the what-it-is line, the price line and both buttons are fully inside the viewport without scrolling.
- **Everywhere**: no console error, no horizontal scroll, nothing past the viewport edge, no clipped text, tap targets of 44px or more at 360, 390 and 768px, focus rings of 2px at 3:1 or better on every control, AA contrast measured from the rendered screens, nothing left invisible after scrolling, and reduced motion (nothing animates, everything works).

## What was found and fixed during this rerun

| # | Found by | Fault | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Contrast check, first trial run | "The online calendar opens shortly." in the last section was light grey on the pale blue band, 1.95:1 | It now takes the page's muted text colour there; measured in the final run |
| 2 | Tap-target check, first trial run | "Close" in the sample call panel was 38px wide | Minimum width 44px |
| 3 | Screenshot review | On desktop "Who answered" wrapped, making a five-line headline that pushed the price and buttons down | Desktop headline size reduced so it sets in four lines; a check for four lines was added to the audit |
| 4 | Screenshot review | The expander still read "15 more" after it was opened | The count hides while the list is open |

One fault was in the audit script, not the page: the "Change my answers" test reused a handle to a button that the page had already re-rendered. The script now finds the button afresh. All results below are from the final full run after these fixes.
