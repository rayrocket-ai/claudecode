Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Every investment purchase should answer three exit questions before the offer, and none of them need a forecast.

1. If you had to sell, who is the likely buyer? Another investor, or a family who would live there?

I do not project appreciation. What we can do is choose a property with more than one way out.

Buy on the decade, not the day. An exit plan is how you hold through the years in between.
