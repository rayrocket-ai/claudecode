Hi {{contact.first_name}},

The biggest worry I hear from move-up sellers is carrying two homes at once.

We plan for it before either home is on the table. There are a few ways to manage the gap:

- Line up the two closing dates so they sit close together
- Write conditions into your purchase offer. A condition is something that must happen before the deal is firm.
- Ask your lender about bridge financing, a short-term loan that covers the gap when you buy before your sale closes

Whether bridge financing fits you is a question for your lender or mortgage broker. I can introduce you to one.

We map these options against your timeline so you know your plan before you list.
