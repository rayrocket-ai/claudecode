# Pre-publish checklist

Run on every piece before it goes to Ray. Write the result in the "Compliance check" field as PASS, or FAIL with the line number.

1. Brokerage name present and prominent.
2. Ray named as registered, with "Broker".
3. No property, address, price or client detail without written consent on file. If unsure, FAIL.
4. Every number has a real source link and the period it covers.
5. No guarantee, promise, "best", "top" or "number one" without proof.
6. No financial, mortgage, tax or legal advice. Perspective only.
7. No em dashes. None of: "just", "I'll just", "I wanted to", "give me a shout".
8. One segment, one call to action, a clear next step.
9. Dari longer than two sentences is marked "Ray to review Dari".
10. Music is original or licensed. AI-made media is flagged as AI-generated.
11. Email only: consent basis named, sender identified with address, unsubscribe present.
12. Nothing uses a tragedy, a private person or a competitor's name to sell.

A FAIL on 1 to 6 or 11 sets Status to "Blocked compliance" and raises an interrupt.
