# RECO advertising rules (Ontario, TRESA)

This is a working checklist, not legal advice. The authority is RECO's advertising guidance, the TRESA Code of Ethics, and eXp Realty's compliance team. [Ray to confirm with the broker of record: anything marked below]

1. Brokerage name: every advertisement clearly and prominently shows the brokerage's registered name. [Ray to confirm: exact registered name, e.g. "eXp Realty, Brokerage"]
2. Ray's name appears as registered with RECO, with his registration category (Broker).
3. Team name: "Ray Homes Real Estate" never appears in a way that could be mistaken for the brokerage.
4. No property is advertised without the owner's written consent. Another brokerage's listing needs that brokerage's written Permission to Advertise.
5. No sold price, address, party name or deal term that identifies a transaction without written consent of the parties. [Ray to confirm current rule on publicly available sold data]
6. Nothing false, misleading or deceptive. No "number one", "top", "best" or award claim without the source, the period and the market stated.
7. No guarantees or promises of price, time or savings.
8. Testimonials are real, consented in writing, and not edited to change meaning.
9. Ads for listings state facts that can be verified (size, taxes, fees) with the source.
10. AI-generated images of a property or a person are labelled, and never shown as the real property.
