# CASL (Canada's anti-spam law) for email and text

Working checklist, not legal advice.

1. Consent before sending. Express consent (they asked to hear from Ray) or implied consent (an existing business relationship: a purchase or contract within 2 years, or an inquiry within 6 months). Keep the proof in HighLevel.
2. Identify the sender in every message: Ray Ahmadi, the brokerage name, a mailing address and a contact method. [Ray to confirm: mailing address to use]
3. A working unsubscribe in every message, honoured within 10 business days. An unsubscribe is permanent until the person opts in again.
4. No misleading subject lines or sender names.
5. Never add a contact to a list because they commented, followed or were found online.
6. Referrals: one message is allowed only if the referrer has a relationship with both sides and is named in the message.
7. An AI drafts. It never sends, imports contacts or changes consent status.
