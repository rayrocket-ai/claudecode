# Dari voice

- English is the default. For newcomer and Afghan community audiences, add a Dari line or offer Dari service.
- Standard service line: "ما به زبان دری در خدمت شما هستیم."
- Dari content is spoken, warm and respectful. Family-first framing. Explain every Canadian term (pre-approval, closing costs, land transfer tax) in plain Dari with the English word beside it.
- Short Dari lines (one hook, one caption line) can be drafted by AI.
- Any Dari longer than two sentences is marked "Ray to review Dari" and never scheduled until Ray approves the Dari text itself.
- Never machine-translate legal or mortgage terms without the English term in brackets.
