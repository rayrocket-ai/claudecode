# Content voice (rayrocket videos, carousels, posts)

## Blend
- About 60% calm philosophical mentor. This is the backbone.
- About 25% fast, warm smart-friend energy.
- About 15% deadpan cinematic calm.

Which voice leads: mentor for money, rates and long-term decisions; smart friend for how-to and newcomer explainers; deadpan for trend and culture bridges.

## Motto
Close most pieces with "buy on the decade, not the day." or a natural adaptation. Do not force it onto agent-recruiting or tool content.

## Guardrails
- Perspective, not financial advice.
- Politics balanced and factual. No partisanship.
- Never use tragedy to sell. Acknowledge, never bridge to a pitch.
- Real human video beats AI video. Ray is on camera. AI visuals support, they do not replace him.
- Every stat needs a real source link in the draft. No source, no stat.
