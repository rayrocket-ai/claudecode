# Content formats and weekly rhythm

| Format | Who makes it | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Short video (45 to 75 seconds) | Ray films from the Daily Video Coach shoot list | 3 a day filmed, best ones posted |
| Long-form YouTube (8 to 12 minutes) | Ray films from the content pack outline | Mondays and Thursdays |
| Carousel (7 to 9 slides) | Content pack text, Graphic Designer in Canva | Mon, Wed, Fri |
| Instagram caption post | Content pack | Mon, Wed, Fri |
| LinkedIn post for agents or investors | Content pack | Mon, Wed, Fri |
| Weekly market email | Email Writer | Wednesdays, drafted for approval |

Existing AI team members this builds on: "rayrocket weekly content brief" (Mondays), "rayrocket content pack" (Mon, Wed, Fri), "Daily Video Coach" (daily). The Marketing OS does not rewrite their work; it takes their output through approval, publishing and measurement.

Video file convention: a finished video ready to post is saved in Google Drive with a title starting "POST " followed by the topic. The Approval Desk picks those up.
