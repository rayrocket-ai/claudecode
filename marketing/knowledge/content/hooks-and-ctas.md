# Hooks and calls to action

## Hook (first 3 seconds)
Stack three things: authority (why Ray), curiosity (what they do not know yet), stake (what it costs them).
- State the stake in plain words. No clickbait that the piece does not pay off.
- One hook per piece. For Dari pieces, write the Dari hook line separately.

## Calls to action by segment
- First-time buyer and newcomer family: the free home plan through the Move Planner.
- Move-up seller: a pricing and move plan conversation.
- Investor: ask for the cash flow sheet.
- Agent: a private conversation about the team, ARIA or PROPEL.

One call to action per piece. A clear next step, never a soft close. Links and keywords come from offer/. If a link is missing there, write [Ray to confirm: link] and do not invent one.
