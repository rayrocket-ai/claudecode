# Metrics and attribution

## What counts
Views and followers are signals. Calls booked and signed clients are the score. Goal: $500k a month; first milestone $125k a month, about $4,100 a day.

## Per post (from Metricool, weekly)
Views, reach, likes, comments, shares, saves. Stored on the post's row in Airtable "MOS Posts".

## Per day (leads and bookings)
New leads, hot leads and calls booked, by source and segment, in Airtable "MOS Leads". Counts only, no contact details. Sources: Viktor's daily HighLevel line in #agent-marketing, lead notification emails in Ray's Gmail as the fallback (counted, never copied), Ad Advisor for Meta lead ads, and Ray or Diana for referrals.

## Deals
Signed clients and closings in Airtable "MOS Deals" with the source and the post or ad credited. Entered by Ray or Diana.

## Crediting a post or ad
1. The lead's own answer to "how did you find Ray" wins.
2. Otherwise the HighLevel source or UTM.
3. Otherwise the keyword they commented or messaged.
4. Otherwise "Unknown". Never guess.

## Morning Brief marketing lines
Pieces waiting on Ray's approval, posts scheduled today, calls booked yesterday, and the top line of the loop feed. Real numbers only; "not tracked yet" when a number is missing.
