# LinkedIn and email

## LinkedIn
Ray's personal profile, connected in Metricool. Audience: agents, investors, business owners. Text posts and document carousels. First line is the hook; no links in the first line.

## Email
- Sent from HighLevel (Ray Homes CRM). HighLevel is the record for contacts, consent and unsubscribes.
- AI drafts; Ray approves; a person or an approved HighLevel workflow sends. An agent never sends a marketing email itself.
- Weekly market email: one topic, one number with its source, one next step. Under 200 words.
- Segments: first-time buyer, newcomer family, move-up seller, investor, agent, past client.
- Every email follows compliance/casl-email.
