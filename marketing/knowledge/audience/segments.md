# Audience segments

Leads come mostly from referrals, Ray's database, and social video (rayahmadi / rayrocket on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube) in English and Dari.

| Segment | Main objection | What they need to hear |
|---|---|---|
| First-time buyer | Can I afford it? Is it the wrong time? | Their real number first; FHSA, RRSP Home Buyers' Plan, Ontario land transfer tax refund; buy on the decade |
| Newcomer family (often Afghan community) | Little Canadian credit history; trust; language | Newcomer mortgage programs, credit building, every step explained, service in Dari |
| Move-up seller | Sell first or buy first? Carrying two homes | Both moves planned together, pricing backed by local sales, bridge financing ready |
| Investor | Will rent cover costs? Are units legal? | Cash flow sheet before the offer, legality and zoning checked, exit plan |
| Real estate agent (team recruiting, ARIA, PROPEL) | Split, control, real support, switching hassle | Icon-led team, AI tools like ARIA, coaching from live deals, eXp revenue share |

Lead tags used in HighLevel: seg-*, concern-*, timing-*. Every piece of content names exactly one segment.
