# Decision styles (from the Move Planner quiz)

- Numbers: lead with the math.
- Proof: lead with real outcomes from people like them. Only real, consented outcomes.
- Speed: lead with the timeline and the call to action.
- Ease: lead with simple steps and what Ray's team handles.

Use one style per piece. In email, match the style on the contact's quiz answer when it exists; otherwise default to Ease for newcomers and Numbers for investors.
