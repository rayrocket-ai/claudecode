# Approval rules

Nothing goes live until Ray approves it. Diana is the backup approver for routine admin only, never for content in Ray's name.

## How approval works
1. The Approval Desk posts each piece in Slack #agent-marketing, one message per piece, with the full text, the media or design link, the channels, and the compliance result.
2. Ray reacts on that message:
   - white_check_mark: approved as written. Schedule it.
   - x: rejected. Do not post. Do not re-propose the same angle.
   - A thread reply with words: an edit request. Revise and post the new version in the same thread for a fresh reaction.
   - clapper: approved to spend creative credits (Higgsfield or ElevenLabs) on this piece.
3. Only a reaction from Ray's own Slack account counts. An AI never adds a reaction, and never treats its own message as approval.
4. No reaction means no. Pieces waiting more than 5 days are marked expired.

## Tiers
- Tier 1 runs and reports, read only.
- Tier 2 drafts; Ray approves.
- Tier 3 hands off to a human.
All publishing, all client-facing messages and all spending are Tier 2 or higher.
