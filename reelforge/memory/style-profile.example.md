# Style profile

Read by the **editor** and **producer** agents before they choose anything. It
outranks their defaults.

This is for the things that are not numbers. Pacing, caption size and zoom
cadence are handled by `playbook.md` and learned automatically — do not
duplicate them here. Put here what a new editor joining your channel would need
to be told on day one.

Copy this file to `style-profile.md` and edit. Delete anything you have no
opinion about; an empty section is better than an invented one.

---

## The channel

What it is about, and who watches it. One or two sentences.

> Example: Ontario real estate, for agents rather than buyers. Viewers are
> working realtors who already know the basics and want the parts nobody
> explains.

## What a good clip sounds like

Hooks that have worked. Be specific — quote real openings if you have them.

> Example: Openings that name a number or contradict something everyone
> believes. "Ninety percent of listings get this wrong" works. "Today I want to
> talk about..." does not.

## Voice

How you sound, and how the captions and metadata should sound.

> Example: Direct, no hype. Never write a title I would not say out loud.
> No "🚨 INSANE" thumbnails.

## Do not

Subjects, phrasings, or footage that must never make it into a clip.

> Example: Never cut a clip that names a client or a specific address. Never
> use a moment where I am reading off-screen — it looks like I lost my place.

## Recurring segments

Anything that appears in most videos and should be treated consistently.

> Example: I close every video with "that's the whole trick" — that line is the
> ending, never cut it, and never use it as a hook.
