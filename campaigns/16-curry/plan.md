# 16 Curry Crescent — Campaign Plan (v1, directed by Ray 2026-07-22)

Playbook A: Listing Exposure — same lean structure as 34 Buttonleaf.
Property: Georgetown (Halton Hills), detached 2-storey, 4+1 bed, 4 bath,
4-car garage, 3000–3500 sqft (~4,500 sqft finished incl. basement),
50 x 118 lot, finished walk-up basement. Listed at $1,588,000 by eXp
Realty (MLS W13503106), 23 days on market as of Jul 22.

## Budget

| Where | Daily | Objective |
|---|---|---|
| Meta (FB + IG, one campaign, automatic placements) | $10 | Leads (instant form) |
| YouTube Shorts | $5 | Views / retargeting pool |
| **Total** | **$15** | |

NOTE: with Buttonleaf also approved at $15/day, total account spend
becomes $30/day when both are live.

## Strategy (mirrors Buttonleaf)

- No price, no address in creative or copy. Instant form trade: "Get the
  price + address + full tour."
- Focus: Georgetown + the house type. This listing has unusually strong
  creative hooks — lean on them instead of price:
  1. The 4-car garage (rare; car people and trades families share this).
  2. In-law suite potential: finished walk-up basement with separate
     entrance, rec room, 3-piece bath.
  3. "The expensive work is already done": $60K professional basement
     waterproofing (2021), new furnace + water tank (2025), roof ~7 yrs.
     Compliance: state as completed work, no warranties implied.
- Geo: Georgetown + 25 km radius — covers Brampton, Milton, Acton, and
  the Mississauga fringe (the Georgetown move-up feeder ring). Broad
  within radius; Housing Special Ad Category declared.
- Form qualifiers: same three (timeline, pre-approval, home to sell).
- 23 days on market already: creative must feel fresh, not stale. Never
  use "still available" framing; use discovery framing.

## Expectations

Same as Buttonleaf: 2–5 leads/week after learning week, CPL target $15,
ceiling $30, YouTube ~100–150 views/day as retargeting-pool growth.

## Kill / decision rules

Identical to Buttonleaf (7-day windows; pause at CPL > $30 after $70
spend; replace at CTR < 0.8% after 2,000 impressions; week-3 YouTube
review). Cross-campaign rule: if one property clearly outperforms the
other on CPL after 2 full weeks, propose shifting budget toward the
winner (Ray approves).

## Compliance

Same checklist as Buttonleaf: Housing SAC, eXp Realty branding, licensed
audio only, no guaranteed claims, fair-housing language check, privacy
policy on form, CASL-compliant follow-up via GHL.
