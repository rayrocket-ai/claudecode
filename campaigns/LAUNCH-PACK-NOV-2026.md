## Part 1. Meta (Facebook + Instagram), per listing

Ads Manager, Create, objective **Leads**.

1. Special Ad Category: **Housing**, country Canada.
2. Campaign budget: off (set it on the ad set). Advantage+ on.
3. Ad set: conversion location **Instant forms**, daily budget **$5.00**,
   location = the listing's city with a **25 km radius** (Meta's housing
   minimum is about 24 km), Advantage+ audience, Advantage+ placements.
4. Instant form: type **Higher intent**. Intro headline "See the full
   details on this home". Questions: full name, email, phone, then the
   three custom questions (When are you looking to buy? / Are you
   pre-approved? / Do you have a home to sell first?). Privacy policy:
   your Ray Homes privacy URL. Thank-you: "Thank you. The full details
   are on the way." Button: Visit Ray Homes, https://www.rayhomes.ca.
5. Ads: put every cut and the carousel in this ONE ad set (6 to 10 ads
   when creative allows). Primary text, headline and description are in
   that listing's plan.md under "Primary text".
6. Leave it PAUSED, check the preview on phone, then publish.

## Part 2. YouTube through Google Ads, per listing

Google Ads account 134-435-0136. Upload each cut to the Ray Homes
YouTube channel as **Unlisted** first, then:

1. New campaign, goal: **Awareness and consideration**, type **Video**
   (if Google only offers Demand Gen, pick Demand Gen and set channel
   controls to YouTube only: Shorts, in-stream, in-feed).
2. Subtype **Video views**. Bidding: **Target CPV**, let Google suggest.
3. Budget **$5.00/day**. Networks: YouTube videos only. Uncheck video
   partners.
4. Locations: add the listing's city with a **25 km radius**, then open
   **Location options** and choose **Presence: people in or regularly in
   your targeted locations**. Exclude **Philippines** and **United
   States**. This is the fix for the spam-lead leak found in the
   September audit.
5. Housing policy (Canada): no age, gender, parental status, marital
   status or postal-code targeting. Leave demographics on "All".
6. Audience: in-market segment **Real Estate > Residential Properties >
   Residential Properties (For Sale)**. Optional custom segment of
   people who searched "homes for sale <city>". If Google flags either
   under the housing policy, remove it and keep geo only.
7. Content exclusions: Standard inventory; exclude embedded videos and
   live streams.
8. Ad: the unlisted video, final URL https://www.rayhomes.ca, call to
   action **Get the price**, headline from the table below. In Audience
   Manager create a **YouTube users: viewed any video as an ad** list for
   retargeting.
9. Ad disclosure: advertiser verification must be complete ("Ads funded
   by Ray Ahmadi") or Google will limit serving.

## Part 4. After launch

- Leads route to GoHighLevel; ARIA Voice calls back within 5 minutes;
  Ray texts price, address and tour link.
- ADMAX judges each listing on 7-day windows: pause an ad at CPL over
  $30 after $70 spent on it; replace at CTR under 0.8% after 2,000
  impressions. After 2 weeks, budget shifts toward the winning listing
  with Ray's approval.
- At $5/day Meta, expect 1 to 3 leads per listing per week once the
  learning week is over. Buttonleaf and Curry ran CPL $11 to $12 at
  $10/day; expect a similar CPL with fewer leads.
