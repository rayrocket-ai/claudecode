## Implementation Checklist

- [ ] **Set up review links**: Generate a direct Google review short link from Google Business Profile. Get the direct Facebook recommendation URL.
- [ ] **Segment your client list**: Tag each past client as Seller, Buyer, or VIP in your email platform.
- [ ] **Set up suppression**: Create an automation rule that removes a contact from this sequence the moment they click a review link or you manually confirm a review was posted.
- [ ] **Personalize merge fields**: Ensure `{{FIRST_NAME}}` and `{{PROPERTY_ADDRESS}}` are populated for each contact.
- [ ] **Schedule send times**: Tuesday through Thursday, 10:00 AM ET tends to perform best for personal/professional emails in the Toronto market.
- [ ] **Track results**: Monitor open rate, click rate, and actual reviews posted per email. Adjust subject lines and send times based on performance.
- [ ] **Post-sequence**: Move non-reviewers into a general client nurture list. Do not send further review requests for at least 6 months.
