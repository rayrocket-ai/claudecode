### Patterns Among Winners

1. **Video-first, education-heavy, personality-driven** — top accounts show their face, share knowledge, limit pure listing content to ~10-15% of posts
2. **Pick 2-3 platforms and go deep** — spreading thin across 5 platforms leads to mediocre content everywhere
3. **Hyperlocal > generic** — neighbourhood-specific content consistently outperforms broad market stats
4. **Consistency is non-negotiable** — 20+ consecutive weeks of weekly posting yields 450% more engagement than sporadic posting
5. **DMs close deals** — Instagram now favours retention, saves, and DMs over likes and comments

### What Doesn't Work

- Only posting "Just Listed" / "Just Sold" — the #1 cited mistake
- Broadcasting without interacting (one-way megaphone)
- Ignoring comments and DMs
- Starting videos with static shots or "Hey guys, so today..."
- Generic, non-local content
- Avoiding video entirely

## Part 3: Content Strategy

### Content Pillars (80/20 Rule: 80% Value, 20% Promotional)

| Pillar | % of Content | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Market Intelligence** | 25% | Weekly GTA stats, BoC rate analysis, Bill C-4 explainers, condo market updates |
| **Neighbourhood/Community** | 25% | Toronto neighbourhood tours, local business spotlights, "What does $X get you in [area]?" |
| **Education/Tips** | 20% | First-time buyer guides, mortgage renewal advice, rent vs. buy, myth-busting |
| **Property Showcases** | 15% | Listing highlights (short-form), before/after staging, quirky features |
| **Personal Brand/BTS** | 15% | Day in the life, client success stories, team culture, deal stories |

### Hot Topics to Cover Right Now (March 2026)

1. **Bill C-4 — GST elimination for first-time buyers** on new homes up to $1M (savings up to $50K, or $130K with Ontario HST match). This is THE topic right now.
2. **Bank of Canada holding at 2.25%** — what it means for spring buyers/sellers
3. **GTA buyer's market** — 5.0 months of supply, 54 days on market, 100K+ sidelined buyers
4. **Mortgage renewal crisis** — 1M+ households renewing at higher rates
5. **Condo market weakness** — average condo price down 8.9% YoY, investment strategy advice
6. **Tariff impacts on construction costs** — CUSMA renegotiation, lumber tariffs
7. **Spring 2026 market predictions** — "Is this the spring everyone's been waiting for?"

### Video Hook Formulas That Work

Use these in the first 3 seconds of every Reel/TikTok:

- **Curiosity:** "Can you guess what $500K gets you in Scarborough right now?"
- **Myth-bust:** "Everyone thinks you need 20% down. Here's the truth."
- **Number-based:** "3 things nobody tells you about buying in the GTA in 2026"
- **POV/Lifestyle:** "POV: You move into this Leslieville townhouse and walk to brunch every Saturday"
- **Bold statement:** "Toronto condos just had their worst month in a decade. Here's what smart buyers are doing."
- **News reaction:** "The BoC just held rates at 2.25%. Here's what that means for your mortgage."

**Rules:**
- Start with movement (walk-in, camera pan, green-screen swipe) — never a static shot
- Bold on-screen text in the first frame (80%+ watch without sound)
- Drop neighbourhood names and specific price points
- Keep Reels 30-60 seconds for optimal engagement

### Trending Audio (March 2026)

- **"Breeze" by KaelGumba** — upbeat, great for home tours
- **"Kiss It Better" by Rihanna** — trending, luxury property walkthroughs
- **"Until I Found You"** — soft piano, "POV: Your Dream Home" format
- **"I Hate to Be the One to Tell You..."** — myth-busting with b-roll

Check [Tokchart](https://tokchart.com/) weekly for current trending sounds.

## Part 5: Cross-Platform Repurposing Workflow

**Record once, repurpose everywhere:**

1. Film a **5-10 min YouTube video** (e.g., "March 2026 Toronto Market Update")
2. Cut into **3-4 clips (30-60 sec)** for Instagram Reels + TikTok + YouTube Shorts
3. Pull key stats into an **Instagram carousel** (infographic style)
4. Write a **LinkedIn post** summarizing the key insight
5. Upload the **full video natively to Facebook**
6. Turn into a **blog post** for SEO (leverage existing blog audience of 23.9K)
7. Use quotes/stats as **Instagram Stories** with polls for engagement

**This multiplies content ROI by 3x while reducing creation time by 60%.**
