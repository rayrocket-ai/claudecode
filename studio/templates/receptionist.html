Receptionist questions, answered

Is it available right now?

It's in early access. Submit the scope form and we'll book a 15-minute call, confirm your quote, and onboard you in waves. Early-access members lock their pricing for 12 months.

Will callers know it's AI?

It sounds natural and conversational. We disclose what's required by law, and any call it can't handle gets transferred to you or takes a detailed message.

What does setup involve?

We script your greeting, load your service areas and listings, set transfer rules, and — depending on plan — connect your calendar and CRM and design your follow-up sequences. The estimate card shows the expected effort for your scope.

Which CRMs do you support?

Follow Up Boss, KvCORE, and most CRMs with an API or email-to-lead intake. Tell us yours in the form and we'll confirm on the scoping call.

Can I keep my existing phone number?

Yes — we forward your existing line (or unanswered calls only) to the receptionist. You stay in control of when it picks up.
